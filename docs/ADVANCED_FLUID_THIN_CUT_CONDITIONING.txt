ADVANCED FLUID FOLLOW-UP — INDEPENDENT THIN-CUT ORACLE
29 October 2026 | Experimental only | Private numerical research, not an unlock

SUMMARY
The Stage10 nonlinear thin-cut disagreement was not simply a defective fast
response cache. The original local QR solve is itself inaccurate for the frozen
least-squares operator. Its 2-norm condition number is at least 3.38528e10.
Independent arithmetic and decomposition establish this for the selected local
patch; it is not a conclusion about all fluid geometries or continuum accuracy.

Production fluid code, Basic Water, M54/M55 and FLU-001 are unchanged. Alpha's
advanced-fluid compile lock remains in force. Solbert's independent research
has not been modified, imported or restarted. No new editor/runtime API exists.

JUSTIFIED PRIVATE CORRECTION
Retain the original local QR and physical constraints. For x from either the
original QR or fast prepared response, compute an accurate gradient
 g = J^T*(r-J*x), then solve
 delta = P*R^-1*R^-T*P^T*g; x <- x+delta,
where J*P=Q*R is the existing QR. This is numerical iterative refinement of the
same constrained least-squares problem, not damping, an energy clamp, additional
physical iterations, a different pressure model or a tiny-cell cutoff.

Two predefined refinement steps were tested. In the thin case the FIRST step
already reduces free-flux error to1.054e-16 from both starting values. After the
second, the160-bit gradient gives flux error below1.952e-22; a paired-binary64
(TwoSum/FMA) gradient gives below8.179e-22 and maximum free-face normal error
below2.0e-13. Axis-aligned and ordinary oblique controls also pass the unchanged
1e-12 ACCURACY bound against the independent oracle.

The separate old-reference EQUIVALENCE comparison remains explicitly FAILED in
the thin case:2.825e-8 versus its unchanged1e-12 bound. Stage10's2131 assertions
established equivalence to the original arithmetic; they did not establish this
new independent local accuracy. No historical threshold or result was changed.

COST AND REJECTED APPROACHES
The160-bit gradient prototype costs47.6–48.0ms per instrumented refinement step
on the selected816x294 thin patch. That is unsuitable as a runtime replacement.
An immutable exact-zero sparse coefficient cache with paired-binary64 arithmetic
reduces those individual instrumented samples to1.63–1.67ms. These times include
correction construction and independent error measurement; they exclude cache
preparation, geometry, global pressure solve and all other patches. They are not
a whole-fluid frame benchmark or a multi-run p95 claim. The axis case samples
.42ms; ordinary oblique samples1.62–2.12ms. No end-to-end speedup is claimed.

Paired arithmetic is a qualified private experiment only: IEEE binary64,
round-to-nearest, std::fma, bounded finite exponent range, Linux64-bit-significand
long double for the existing local QR. Windows/binary64 native QR portability,
other ranks/conditioning regimes and general arithmetic coverage are unproven.
No fast-math changes or native physics scalar changes were made.

Two genuine compile failures are preserved: mixed160/256-bit auto declarations,
and an unsupported Eigen permutation-product addTo expression. Both were fixed
by explicit independent temporaries. Earlier failed and diagnostic variants are
archived, not replaced by the final positive log.
