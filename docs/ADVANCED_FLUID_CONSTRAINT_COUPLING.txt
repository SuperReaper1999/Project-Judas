What was established
--------------------
1. A foreign pressure calculation must distinguish physical velocity from a
   native constraint warm-start iterate. The retained incremental-only trial
   reads +100 m/s of temporary support warm-start motion as genuine wall motion
   and raises a 4,000 J reduced fluid/body system to 40,000,000 J. Matching
   two-sided accumulated pressure impulses removes that artificial work.
   This is a demonstrated NEW-backend integration hazard, not proof that this
   exact path caused the original production PBF failure.
2. Correct dual bookkeeping alone is insufficient: free-body inverse mass is
   not the response of a body constrained by contacts/joints. With 8,000 kg of
   reduced fluid against an 80 kg supported body, the old two-round composition
   retains an actual interface residual of 0.980296 m/s.
3. A pressure solve using contact geometry but publishing only pressure is also
   rejected. Its two-body support chain reaches 12,500 J from 4,000 J. Native
   contact velocities and their accumulated impulses must agree with the coupled
   solution. Increasing native iteration budgets was not the correction.
4. The private batch instead solves and publishes pressure, native contact
   reactions and prepared joint reactions together from the physical predictor,
   before ordinary warm-start application. It retains native CCD, integration,
   geometric correction, contact/joint budgets and final observations.

Mathematical correction
-----------------------
The reference projects predicted fluid/rigid velocity in the physical mass
metric subject to incompressibility, solid non-penetration, normal continuity
and the participating native joint constraints. Native contact impulses remain
nonnegative. A known-supported body is never assigned infinite mass by policy.

For bilateral rigid constraints J v + b = 0, let B B^T = M_r^-1 and A = J B.
Derive q0 = -A^T (A A^T)^dagger (J v* + b), and P = I - A^dagger A.
Then v_bar = v* + B q0, and the pressure response is -B P B^T G_r^T p.
The implementation uses a rank-aware, reorthogonalized mass-whitened row basis;
no artificial stiffness or regularization is inserted. All participating bodies
share this response, including cross-body translation/rotation coupling.
Joint reactions are recovered from the same Jacobians. Dependent rows receive
a minimum-norm reaction distribution, checked against the full physical wrench.

Generalized rigid coordinates remain rigid coordinates; they are not fluid
particles, independent wall samples or new integration owners. This also avoids
an invalid earlier trial that appended rigid-only rows to a pressure factor whose
base factorization requires nonempty fluid rows. That rejection is preserved.

Scope of contact handling: the small mixed reference exercises unilateral
contact/lift-off. The 3D side-wall reference accepts its derived four-point active
set only after all contact impulses are proven nonnegative. It is NOT a general
3D friction/active-set implementation or support for all joint types/drives.

Remaining work / release boundary
---------------------------------

backend is not wired into FluidWorld/ProductionFluidCoupling or a playable demo.
The private bridge accesses solver rows only to establish the required numerical
contract; it is not a proposed supported raw-pointer API and must not be shipped.

* A bounded native participant/constraint interface with real owner identities,
  wake/island registration, supported friction/limits/motors and correct cache/
  reaction publication. Never select owners by position as the private fixture
  does, or process every world body indiscriminately.

  and changing-contact boundaries, with an explicit fail-closed policy.

  runtime quantity ledger; qualifying failure atomicity rather than pretending

* Actual original Terrain-planet failure, float/sink/waves/transfer/application
  regressions, practical memory/performance and a human gameplay re-test.
