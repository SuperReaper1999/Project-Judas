Demonstrated mechanisms and rejected approaches:
* Real native float pose displacement differs from integrated instantaneous
  velocity flux by1.073236e-8m3 in the original moving-piston trial. Geometric
  swept volume and physical donor momentum must be handled as distinct inputs.
  Neither body pose nor authoritative owned volume is repaired to hide that.
* A fully flooded guided fixture needs impermeability at its wetted solid
  boundaries. The earlier unilateral fixture did not demonstrate its requested
  local pressure energy property. This does not invalidate unilateral separation
  in models which actually support detachment/cavitation.
* Tiny native joint angular residuals can still produce nonrepresentable cut
  slivers. The bounded piston reference condenses its authored slider mobility
  analytically; it is not generic joint-reaction or free-six-DOF integration.
* Instantaneously filling the entire 1m above-plate bay is REJECTED. As the donor
  opening first appears, its height is O(dt); whole-width levelling demands
  O(1/dt) velocities. The rejected input produced ~3547m/s transport wind while
  physical source speeds were below.2m/s. A finite incoming front instead grows
  with the actual represented front/donor velocity. Its rational storage geometry
  is evaluated throughout the interval, not merely interpolated at endpoints.
* Direct rectangular QR with an explicit11-dimensional trace-free cell space
  avoids normal-equation conditioning and trace leakage, but small point-normal
  residuals still do not guarantee transport accuracy in newborn thin cells.
  A1.33881e-8m-wide front failed matrix GCL at1.08e-4 despite normal residuals near
  8e-11. Shared face-flow ownership in a conforming RT0 kinematic wind removes
  this mismatch by construction, with independent connected-component gauges.

  GCL. Direct QR basis normalization did NOT fix it (1.16e-9); five-point temporal
  quadrature did NOT fix it (2.54e-9). Both rejected experiments are retained.
  ReferenceAleTransport instead assembles the conservative weak operator directly

  original1e-10 conservation/energy gates, inserting damping or repairing volume.
