Transport progress and scope
----------------------------
DG1 advection on an immutable fully filled mesh passes13 checks, including
independent P/L/energy integration and third-order time refinement. Positive
wet-face flux integrates sign-changing affine velocity rather than dropping
outflow merely because net flux is negative (46 checks). Incoming wet-state
initialization preserves actual donor momentum/angular momentum/energy for its
qualified constant-flow cases (16 checks).

Moment reconstruction preserves the authoritative volume and minimizes the
centroid defect; it refuses a translated detached slab that no single cut can
represent. Splitting the spatial representation fixes that specific geometric
case. It is NOT a general adaptive refinement implementation. Primary mathematical
reference: V.Dyadechko/M.Shashkov, Moment-of-fluid interface reconstruction,
LA-UR-05-7571, https://cnls.lanl.gov/~shashkov/papers/main.pdf.
Only the reconstruction principle is investigated; no paper repair algorithm
or independent Solbert solver was imported.

One pressure-to-quantity interval passes80 checks: calm, height-difference flow,
dry-neighbour rewetting and a truly blocking slab, with unchanged ledger totals
and nonnegative cells. Long pressure-wave/free-surface evolution is NOT proved.

MaterialMoments adds exact affine-field integration over prepared fragments and
an L2 projection onto their unchanged union.412 checks cover actual1000->200 L
restriction and100 successive state-fed refill/projection cycles per gravity
frame, retaining represented affine modes to about6e-17 m/s. Incoming material
carries its own velocity; a different incoming field changes momentum and may
lose energy through the measured affine projection. No energy repair is applied.
This is NOT a100-cycle dynamic-pressure refill test: geometry fragments are
prepared independently, no motion/topology inference is claimed, and disconnected
components must be submitted separately. General remapping/advection still needs
qualification before production integration.
