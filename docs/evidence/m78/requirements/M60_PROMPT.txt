GOAL
Extend existing Judas audio with genuine bounded-memory streaming, motion-based
Doppler, geometry-driven obstruction/occlusion, and authored environmental reverb.
Provide the editor, public JavaScript and streamed-world integration needed for
ordinary games to use them. This is practical game audio, not acoustic CFD.

Judas owns playback, spatial/DSP primitives and resource lifetime.
Projects own sounds, routing and environment settings.
JavaScript decides why music, effects or other sounds play.

## 1. Extend the current audio system

Trace AudioSystem, emitter/listener bindings, ResourceManager, JobSystem, current
JudasJS audio controls and M59 region registration/suspension/destruction.
Reuse their working paths. EngineHost's audio device/service remains independent
of Renderer; runtime worlds own their voice and environment bindings. Do not
create a device, mixer, listener selection policy or audio world per region.

The M34 baseline used miniaudio 0.11.23. Verify the current pin and configuration.
Prefer its existing decoding, spatialization, filters and effect infrastructure;
inspect the pinned implementation, not just current online examples. Do not
replace the backend or upgrade it speculatively. A small pinned permissively
licensed reverb implementation is acceptable if required. Record provenance,
license and exported notices for any addition.

Preserve ordinary whole-clip WAV/MP3/FLAC playback, independent voices, existing
attenuation, full listener orientation, one-shots and loops. New effects should
be opt-in/compatibly defaulted, not silently change every historical sound.
A single active listener is sufficient. Secondary/render-texture cameras must
not create listeners or tick audio again.

## 2. Real audio streaming, not asynchronous whole-file loading

Support an authored buffered-versus-streamed loading policy through normal audio
assets/emitters. Long music and spatial ambience must start after bounded
prefill and continue reading/decoding incrementally. Stream the existing
WAV/MP3/FLAC formats where their decoder supports it; reject any unsupported
combination explicitly. Additional codecs are not a requirement.

Decoded stream memory must be bounded by configured buffering and active voices,
not track duration. Reading the entire encoded file before playback is not the
intended streaming solution either. Two users of the same asset share immutable
metadata but keep independent stream cursors and playback state.

Expose coherent loading/ready/playing/paused/seeking/ended/failed information,
using the existing API style. Support play, pause/resume, stop/restart, looping,
seek and playback position/duration queries. Unknown duration is explicit, not a
fabricated zero. Document seconds versus audio frames, sample-rate conversion,
seek granularity and authored pitch behaviour. Readiness, EOF and temporary
buffer starvation are distinct states.

Looping must cross buffer boundaries without an engine-inserted gap for a
loop-compatible fixture. Do not promise sample-exact compressed-codec seeking
or universal gapless MP3 unless demonstrated. Rapid replacement/seek requests
must reject stale completions instead of replaying old pages.

Keep short effects buffered. Repeated shots must have independent playback where
requested, subject to a bounded documented voice policy; do not globally share
one decoder cursor or turn each shot into a heavyweight disk stream.

## 3. Audio-thread, worker and clock boundaries

Perform file I/O, decoding, initialization and expensive destruction outside the
audio callback. Reuse Judas jobs or deliberately integrate bounded backend jobs;
no uncontrolled extra worker pool. Keep page/decoder operations ordered per
stream, generation-safe and fair enough that one long file cannot starve others.

The callback consumes prepared audio/control state and performs bounded DSP.
No JS, physics queries, live-world traversal, GL, filesystem access, logging,
WaitAll, per-block heap churn or waits on gameplay/worker completion. Use safe
command/parameter handoff and acknowledged retirement. Audit backend locks/waits;
do not claim strict lock-freedom merely because our wrapper uses atomics.

IMPORTANT backend trap: inspect stream uninitialization. miniaudio's data-stream
uninit can wait for a queued cleanup job. Ordinary M59 region unloading must not
block behind that operation. Detach/fade the voice, retain its resources until
safe, and retire asynchronously where necessary. Never wait on the same worker
needed to complete that retirement. Bound and report pending retirement; do not
solve lifetime safety by leaking streams or permanently pinning empty regions.

Underruns must produce defined safe output, not garbage or a stalled callback.
Document whether the media cursor pauses or advances through starvation; count
underruns and recover without falsely reporting EOF. Invalid assets fail with
useful diagnostics. A missing audio device must not crash the game.

Audio runs on its sample clock, not once per physics step. Frame updates publish
spatial/control state; a 0/1/multiple-fixed-step frame must not duplicate playback.
Backend completion/error notifications are queued to a safe main-thread boundary
if exposed to scripts. Never call QuickJS from the mixer/decoder thread. Do not
promise sample-exact audiovisual or deterministic network synchronization.

## 4. Doppler, orientation and motion

Add opt-in Doppler using real source/listener motion, not distance alone or a
scripted pitch curve. Use one Doppler calculation: backend or Judas-owned,
not both. Compose it explicitly with authored pitch.

Positions, directions and velocities must use one consistent world/reference
frame. Reuse the current presented-pose path for smooth spatial presentation;
obtain trustworthy velocity from physics/CharacterMotor/camera tracking where
available. Attached emitters/listeners need point velocity, including angular
motion at their offset. For purely scripted motion, accept explicit generic
velocity or use a documented finite-difference fallback with a coherent clock.

Initialization, camera switches, teleports and region placement must reset any
motion estimate that would otherwise create a huge pitch spike. Do not estimate
velocity from the asset-local position of one frame and world position of another.
Transform region-local anchors exactly once. Full listener orientation supplies
panning axes; gravity is not an audio up vector.

Use metres/seconds and a documented sound speed/Doppler scale. Define the assumed
acoustic-medium rest frame: stationary uniform air in the simulation frame is
sufficient. Source/listener velocities must be relative to that same medium.
A change of coordinate frame must transform the medium too. Do not claim exact
acoustics from only the magnitude of relative velocity.

Coincident positions, zero Doppler and extreme velocities remain finite under a
declared subsonic/game approximation. Do not simulate shock waves or alter
physical body velocities to protect audio. 2D/UI/music voices bypass spatial
Doppler unless deliberately configured otherwise.

Prove stationary, approaching, receding, co-moving and rotated configurations,
plus a rotating offset emitter and a discontinuous view change. Preserve M23's
fixed double origin/local-float contract; no live rebasing or atmosphere rewrite.

## 5. Geometry-aware obstruction/occlusion

Use the existing PhysicsWorld/M44 geometry and filtering to determine whether
eligible spatial sources have a clear path to the listener. Evaluate queries
outside the audio thread at a bounded control cadence. Source selection/query
budgets should use active/audible demand with stable ordering, not render visibility.

One or a small fixed set of rays is adequate initially. Turn the obstruction
result into smoothed attenuation and low-pass filtering. The actual DSP must
change, not just a displayed occlusion flag. Keep distance attenuation, authored
gain and occlusion separately observable and avoid applying the same gain twice.

Reuse M39 collision/query masks and ignored handles. Exclude source/listener
geometry appropriately, skip sensors unless opted in, and handle legitimate
geometry hits with no entity wrapper. Render layers do not decide whether a wall
blocks sound. A moved/opened door changes the result through its actual collider,
not a hardcoded Door/Shooter/Room special case.

Skipped updates retain/interpolate a known result rather than abruptly toggling.
Reset stale observations on destruction/replacement. Clearly document query
latency, thin/grazing geometry limits and the use of currently resident geometry.
Do not synchronously load missing regions merely to trace a sound.

This is approximate obstruction, not diffraction, transmission through arbitrary
materials or a geometric acoustic propagation solver.

## 6. Authored environmental reverb

Provide real bounded algorithmic reverb, not a volume boost or a canned echo
recording. Prefer existing permissive DSP rather than inventing a research reverb.
Use reusable settings/assets and ordinary environment-zone components with
explicit shape/frame, priority, blend distance, wet/send amount and the supported
decay/damping parameters. No inferred room names or hardcoded presets in C++.

Support at least two audibly different authored environments and a dry/default
outside. Listener-weighted zones with per-emitter sends are an acceptable initial
approximation; state that this is not portal-connected multi-room acoustics.
Overlaps resolve deterministically from authored/stable identity, never whichever
streaming job finished last. Zone orientation/placement must work off world-Y.

Share a bounded number of reverb processors/sends rather than allocate one large
reverb per voice. Avoid doubling the dry signal. Smooth transitions; do not
reinitialize delay buffers every frame. Parameter changes must be safe for the
selected DSP and preserve/transition tails without obvious bursts or clicks.

A stopped source may leave a legitimate bounded tail. Region/zone removal must
fade or retire that tail safely without retaining live entity pointers or leaving
effects on the next world. Full Stop/project replacement clears runtime DSP state.

## 7. Minimal routing, fades and pause policy

Add/reuse only the routing needed to make these features usable: master output
and project-defined sound groups/buses with gain, mute, pause and smooth fades.
Music/effects/UI are demo routing choices, not engine-required game categories.
Keep environment sends and per-emitter effect bypass understandable.

Projects must be able to pause gameplay sounds while keeping chosen music/menu
sounds running, or pause them all. JavaScript/M41 UI chooses the policy. Pausing
must not repeatedly restart sources or accumulate scene events for a burst on
resume. Use the audio clock for audible fades even when fixed simulation is paused.

No interactive music framework, sequencer or mixer graph editor. Two ordinary
voices and fades are enough to demonstrate a music transition if useful.

## 8. M59 ownership and lifecycle integration

Audio streaming and world streaming are different kinds of streaming. A voice
must retain the audio data it needs without making an entire region immortal.
Use M59's ordinary registration, demand, adoption and dependency contracts.

Required cases:
- Persistent-root music continues through additive gallery loads/unloads without
  restarting or duplicating, including independent requests for the same region.
- Region-local ambience activates once, deactivates cleanly and releases decoder,
  buffer, query and zone resources after safe retirement.
- A carried/adopted sound-emitting entity keeps its existing voice and position
  across ownership transfer; unloading its birthplace does not kill it.
- Cancelled preparation and late completions cannot resurrect unloaded sources.
- Repeated outbound/backtracking trips leave no stuck-unloading regions, stale
  sends, unintended pins or increasing voice/buffer counts.

Do not let loading a region silently replace the active listener. Distinguish
suspension from destruction. Preserve M59's documented restart-on-resume policy
for ordinary suspended region sounds unless explicitly improving and testing it;
lossless decoder/DSP snapshot suspension is not required. Persistent music is
owned by the retained root, not saved and restored on every boundary crossing.

Whole-scene replacement, reload, Play/Stop and process shutdown have explicit
cleanup ordering. Script destroy callbacks may issue valid final audio commands
while their world service still exists; commands are invalid after teardown.
Do not build M61 disk saves or serialize backend pointers, decoder heaps or tails.

## 9. Editor, resources and JudasJS

Integrate loading policy, relevant emitter/listener controls, routing and reverb
zones with current inspector authoring, stable assets, scene serialization,
prefab overrides/remapping, runtime spawning and composed-region placement.
Use invariant serialized numbers regardless of M58 display locale. Playback
cursors and diagnostic counters are runtime state, not authored fingerprints.
Follow current schema/conditional-field conventions without gratuitous bumps.

Extend existing public audio access rather than replacing it with an incompatible
API. Expose enough safe control/query data for streaming readiness, seek/time,
looping, fades/groups, Doppler/occlusion settings and environments. Provide useful
errors, units, optional-result semantics and validity/lifetime documentation.
Do not expose raw miniaudio objects or require project scripts to poll private
native dispatch. Validate parameters and protect callback/handle lifetimes.

Update judas.d.ts, reference/landing page, machine inventory, live enumeration,
drift checking and an executed cookbook example. Update current architecture
and M59's coverage matrix only where M60 actually changes the contract.

Export every referenced audio/effect/settings dependency through normal package
resolution, including long streamed files and notices. Run after moving the
package, from unrelated CWD and read-only installation. No network service,
repository fallback or file writes beside installed assets are required.

## 10. One clear listening lab and a real game integration

Provide an ordinary small audio project with safe initial volume and straightforward
JS/M35/M41 controls. Original or clearly redistributable assets only. Use an
actual long audio file for streaming proof, not a procedural oscillator hidden
behind a streaming label. Short generated signals are fine for numerical tests;
do not commit giant raw test recordings or soak-test output.

The lab should make each capability easy to hear in isolation: streamed music
with pause/seek/loop; a moving tone/source for Doppler; a source behind a wall and
openable physical door; dry and contrasting reverb spaces; independent routing
controls. Include a rotated-frame case in a focused fixture or the lab, not a
whole new planetary game. Display readiness and simple measured audio diagnostics.
A dry/bypass comparison is useful; it must bypass actual DSP, not swap recordings.

Also integrate the features into Streamed Spring Range with minimal content changes:
persistent-root streamed music, region-local ambience, physical-wall/door
occlusion, environment transitions and a travelling sound-emitting prop. Existing
shots/hits/UI remain usable; avoid duplicate upgraded and legacy playback paths.
Keep chase, score, camera, carrying and door decisions in JS. Preserve M58 locale
switching and the M59 return journey. Do not redesign gameplay, navigation or water.

## 11. Focused proof, then one candidate gate

Use the real production decoding/mixing path with device-free PCM capture where
appropriate. A control flag changing is not proof that the sound changed.
Headless signal measurements establish mechanics, not human listening acceptance.

Keep focused coverage around these behaviours rather than a huge audio matrix:

1. Long file begins after bounded prefill, memory plateaus across repeated pages,
   and a longer equivalent track does not require proportionally more PCM memory.
   Exercise normal WAV/MP3/FLAC paths without testing every codec combination.
2. Two voices share an asset but independently play/pause/seek/loop. Page/loop
   transitions, EOF and rapid seek/replacement do not emit stale data.
3. Deliberately delayed decode demonstrates safe underrun recovery; cancel,
   missing/corrupt input and queued teardown cannot crash or stall the mixer.
4. Captured tones show the expected stationary/approach/recede/co-moving Doppler
   direction and approximate ratio. Rotated/far-origin and teleport cases are safe.
5. A real collider causes gain/high-frequency changes; moving it restores output.
   Filtering, self-ignore and physics-only occluders behave as documented.
6. An impulse produces a real decaying wet tail, bypass is dry, zone overlap and
   removal are deterministic, and repeated settings changes remain finite.
7. Bus/fade/pause controls affect actual output independently; UI sounds can keep
   playing while chosen gameplay sounds pause, with no resume burst.
8. Repeated region trips, demand cancellation, adoption, source/zone destruction
   and slot reuse retire only the right voices. Persistent music keeps one cursor.
9. Project scripts use public APIs; declarations, inventory and examples match.
   Locale changes do not affect audio-setting serialization or gameplay state.
10. Editor authoring/prefabs/Play-Stop, whole-world reload, device-free failure
    handling and a moved/read-only export all work through normal paths.

Test real-time device playback briefly as well as offline DSP. Do not change OS
volume, close the operator's applications or monopolize CPU/GPU for stress tests.
After completion run one clean Release build, one production suite, actual async
integration, focused M60 checks, affected M59/control regressions, API/cookbook
checks and editor/standalone/export smoke. During development use focused checks.
Narrow late corrections get affected reruns, not another full gate by ritual.
Preserve genuine failure evidence and distinguish original versus final results.

## 12. M56 performance and boundedness

Use existing profiler scopes/counters for preparation/decode workers, main-thread
spatial/occlusion/zone work and resource retirement. Export lightweight audio
counters safely; do not insert allocating profiler scopes or global locking into
the callback merely to time it. Measure DSP cost offline or through appropriate
bounded device diagnostics, and label what was actually measured.

Report buffered versus streaming memory, stream prefill/seek latency, underruns,
voices, decoded buffer high-water marks, active reverb processors, query counts,
pending retirements and region pin reasons. Distinguish owned bytes from OS caches
and process RSS. Include maximum indivisible unload/retirement work.

Compare a small useful workload: no audio, ordinary buffered effects, then music
plus spatial/occluded voices and reverb. Around 8 and 32 voices are enough for a
sanity check. Capture frame median/p95/max and audio/control/DSP cost separately;
CPU submission time is not device latency. Use the current i7-4790/GTX 970-class
machine and disclose concurrent applications without demanding they be stopped.

With optional acoustics/streaming unused, ordinary small games should not pay
for a large reverb bank, continuing occlusion queries or needless workers. Stop
must drain resources safely. Avoid optimizing unrelated engine systems.

## 13. Scope limits

No sound-propagation PDE, diffraction research, acoustic ray tracing, automatic
room reconstruction, portal-acoustics graph, baked acoustic probes, convolution
IR authoring suite, HRTF/binaural framework, mandatory new codecs, voice chat,
microphone recording, networking, interactive-music framework or full audio editor.
No automatic atmosphere/vacuum/underwater physics coupling; generic filter/gain
controls can support later project choices without altering M54/M55.
No multiple-device/listener redesign, broad decoder rewrite, M61 save system or
unrelated physics/render/navigation changes. Keep current platform scope.

Approximate occlusion and reverb are acceptable when audibly useful and honestly
bounded. Do not advertise a real acoustic simulation or claim listening from PCM
numbers. Inspect the pinned backend if a proposed operation is unavailable; report
an actual blocker rather than silently dropping a core requirement or repeatedly
hammering a failing approach.

## 14. Human review and delivery

Provide controls and ONE concise checklist covering:
- Start the long track; pause/resume, seek, loop and adjust group gain.
- Hear a source approach/pass/recede; compare Doppler off/on.
- Walk behind a wall, open the door and hear the obstruction change smoothly.
- Move between dry/reverberant spaces; compare wet/dry and listen for clean tails.
- Play Streamed Spring Range across several galleries AND back: music continues,
  local sounds clean up, and a carried emitter survives its original region unload.
- Pause, switch locale, resume, reload and Stop; no stuck controls or ghost audio.
- Inspect M56 buffering/query/retirement diagnostics and repeat in moved standalone.

The operator owns auditory/gameplay acceptance. Do not mark it passed yourself.
