## 1. The consumer problem and the scope

Claude's Skate & Parkour cart currently moves by setting a static body's transform.
It can follow a route, but does not provide the physical pushing/contact behaviour
wanted from a moving scripted object. Moving platforms and other prescribed-motion
objects need the same generic capability.

Deliver an explicit kinematic motion authority: JS commands motion; Judas advances
that motion through its authoritative physics timeline; dynamic bodies respond to
contacts. Do not implement a native golf cart, skitching, vehicle AI or route system.

Conner also reports that the editor scene view only seems to orbit a fixed point:
panning is either absent, broken or undiscoverable. Establish which before changing it.

Not included: new vehicle dynamics, balance/get-up, retargeting, multiplayer,
lighting APIs, Android, a broad contact-solver optimization campaign, or a new editor
camera framework. Do not add moving concave triangle-mesh support under this milestone.

## 2. Inspect and extend the existing authority model

Read the current motion/body types, rigid-motion sampling and CCD, contact solver,
broadphase, sleeping, CharacterMotor/support frames, script transform setters,
M70 animated boundary anchors, authoring and persistence paths.

The consumer report does not prove that Judas has no internal kinematic machinery.
Reuse or generalize suitable existing mechanisms rather than adding a second physics

Static, dynamic and kinematic authority must be explicit. Kinematic motion is

Dynamic neighbours still receive ordinary contact response. Do not claim closed-system
momentum conservation when an externally driven body supplies work.

## 3. Real prescribed motion, not disguised teleportation

Provide public, bounded pose-target and velocity-driven control through the existing
component/API style. Choose exact names after inspecting current declarations.

- A pose command advances toward its position/orientation over a defined fixed
  interval. It must not jump straight to the endpoint before collision processing.
- Velocity control has explicit linear/angular units, frames and continuation rules.
- Define replacement, stop, missing-command and multiple-writes-in-one-step behaviour.
  Do not integrate a target and a velocity as two competing commands.
- Keep explicit placement/teleport distinct from continuous kinematic motion. Preserve
  existing transform-setter semantics; do not silently reinterpret old game code.
- Validate complete requests before publication: finite data, valid rotations, body
  identity/type, supported shape and usable coordinates. Invalid requests leave a
  coherent previous state and useful diagnostics.

Linear and angular velocity must agree with the actual prescribed trajectory and
bone/body/COM conventions. Contact-point velocity must account for rotation, not just
translation of the entity origin. Document target rotation interpolation, quaternion
sign/wrap handling, and how continuous spins are commanded without wrap discontinuities.

Use fixed authoritative samples, never render delta time or the last camera's matrix.
Presentation may interpolate the result but must not create motion history or change
collisions. Preserve M70's represented-rotation correction: unchanged/sub-ULP angular
updates must not manufacture alternating rotations and reopen contacts indefinitely.

Keep large positions in Judas's existing representation; form small relative offsets
before float calculations. No universal world-up, extra gravity policy, origin rebasing
or duplicated reference-frame conversion.

## 4. Collision and support behaviour

Use ordinary broadphase, continuous motion/contact processing, filters, triggers and
queries. A moving kinematic body must wake and interact with sleeping dynamic bodies
when appropriate. An unchanged command must not manufacture movement or contact churn.

Demonstrate pushing with real contact impulses, including contact generated during
rotation and motion that would cross a small dynamic object within one step. Do not
fake the result by parenting, directly moving the crate, cancelling its velocity,
disabling collision or using an endpoint-overlap test as the whole movement model.

Document the actual policy for kinematic-versus-static/kinematic obstruction and for
objects trapped by prescribed motion. A commanded trajectory is not automatically a
collision-free route. Do not silently promise both unconditionally prescribed motion
and automatic stopping at every obstacle. Any obstruction information must use generic
engine mechanisms; gameplay decides how its door/cart/platform responds.

Support the currently valid moving shape types and diagnose unsupported configurations.
M64 static concave meshes remain static-only. Keep valid existing contacts, masks,
triggers and M70 owner-contact subscriptions working without duplicate callbacks or
stale body identities.

CharacterMotor must remain usable on translating, lifting and rotating supports.
Carry support motion through the existing support/reference-frame contract, not a
second per-frame entity offset. A dynamic crate on the same platform moves through
ordinary contact/friction, not scripted attachment.

Test walking, jumping and leaving the support. Linear and rotational support velocity
must be inherited according to the documented existing contract, without being added
twice. Preserve free movement and loss of support; do not glue riders to the platform.
Use a rotated-gravity/reference setup to expose hidden world-up assumptions.

## 6. Scene-view panning: investigate first, fix only the demonstrated gap

- If absent: add conventional camera-relative panning through the existing controller.

checks can prove camera/pivot updates; Conner judges actual desktop usability.
