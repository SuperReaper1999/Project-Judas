MOVING WET GEOMETRY — BOUNDED NUMERICAL REFERENCE

Scope
-----
This is independent engine-side numerical development, not imported Solbert
research, a replacement FluidWorld, or an Alpha-enabled feature. Production PBF,
M54/M55 and BasicWater sources are unchanged. Original PBF launch/conservation
failures remain preserved and uncorrected in runtime.

1. Geometric quantity and material momentum are different unknowns.
An integrated volume flux comes from actual swept geometry. Incoming momentum
is the integral of the donor physical velocity over that material; do not scale
velocity to repair a quantity discrepancy. Native single-precision pose endpoints
and instantaneous projected velocity differ slightly. The quantity owner follows
the real swept endpoint, never teleports the body or patches water volume.

2. Finite-front storage, not instantaneous levelling.
Let H be below-plate depth, T its top, R right free-surface height, w finite
incoming width and G front gap. The extruded incoming trapezoid has
  Vjet = (w/2) * (G + R - T),
  H + R + Vjet = Vowned,
  R-T = [Vowned - 2H - thickness - wG/2] / (1+w/2).
With w(t)=w0+U*t, the storage height is rational in time. Endpoint interpolation
alone is not the same geometry. Use analytic trajectory and derivative callbacks.
Filling a whole 1 m width immediately through an opening of O(dt) requires
velocities O(1/dt), independently of the true donor velocity. The rejected whole-
plate fixture reached ~3547 m/s transport wind with source speeds below .2 m/s.
That is a model error, NOT evidence of physical fluid motion or a pressure fix.
A finite front advances only with the represented physical front/donor velocity.
Its current convex-prism representation explicitly rejects retreat/detachment/
solid contact needing new topology preparation. It is not a general free-surface
reconstruction algorithm.

3. Moving DG transport reference.
Use an affine spatial field on each tetrahedron, constant in time over an
interval. Physical-coordinate basis functions are fixed within the interval;
normalization uses both endpoint geometries so zero-old-mass cells need no fake
old velocity. The conservative weak operator satisfies
  Mnew - Mold + A + A^T - J = 0,
where J is the nonnegative upwind jump form. Backward-in-time material transport
therefore has the independently checked energy identity
  Enew - Eold + temporal_jump_loss + upwind_loss = 0.
This is specified discretization loss, not added damping or an energy clamp.
Mass-matrix GCL, constant-state conservation, material momentum, equation residual
and energy identity are checked before publishing fields. No skew-symmetrizing or
post-solve volume repair is used. A prescribed advector w different from carried
physical velocity u adds the measured angular term integral(w cross u); do NOT
claim exact Euler angular conservation for that approximation.

4. Thin cells need compatible representations.
Direct rectangular QR improves on pressure normal equations, and an explicit
11-coordinate divergence-free fluid nullspace avoids leaking tiny trace errors
through thin-cell conditioning. But approximate continuity between independently
solved point velocities is still amplified by dt/thickness. The captured first
finite-front geometry has w=1.33881e-8 m and Vjet=7.58791e-12 m3. A QR normal
residual around 8.15e-11 can coexist with a 1.08e-4 matrix-GCL error.
ConformingAleWind instead owns one integrated relative flow per shared face.
RT0 basis on tetrahedron K, opposite vertex x_i:
  r_i(x) = (x-x_i)/(3 |K|),
whose outward integral flux on face i is one and on the other faces is zero.
  w = mesh_velocity + sum_i q_i r_i,
  sum_i q_i = -d|K|/dt.
A maximum-area spanning tree eliminates continuity with one independently checked
gauge per connected component. Remaining cycle flows minimize physical L2 distance
to the supplied velocity predictor. There is no tunable iteration increase,
regularization, artificial damping or change to carried physical velocity.
This lower-order transport-wind approximation is NOT a generic high-order Euler
solver: RT0 relative flow has fewer modes than full affine BDM1. Vortical/advection
accuracy, scalable sparse solution and coupled general-moving boundaries remain
qualification requirements before choosing a production discretization.
The exact same thin case passes full GCL at ~1.26e-12. Reducing width to
4.16667e-13 m still passes unchanged matrix/energy checks; no minimum-width clamp.

5. Ownership and failure boundaries.
Independent subcells have distinct IDs within one voxel. Direct same-voxel flow
is forbidden; legal water reaches the upper pocket only through a genuinely wet
neighbour face. Capacity ownership must be proven disjoint. This bounded reference
currently accepts axis-separated subcell bounds and explicitly rejects unproven
interlocking capacities rather than double-counting them. Transaction failure
preserves old owner/field/front state. Native world advancement itself is NOT
rolled back by these prototypes; an after-substep failure can leave rigid state
advanced. A safe production prepare/publication/failure policy is still required.

6. Second cancellation mechanism and reference-coordinate correction.
The smaller-gap native front (gap4.21703e-6, width3.59419e-7m) still failed the
physical-coordinate material matrix at1.04616e-9. Factoring endpoint quadrature
with QR instead of covariance Cholesky gave1.16352e-9. Five-point temporal Gauss
integration gave2.54164e-9. These do NOT support a conditioning-factor or
under-integration fix; both experiments are rejected and retained.
ReferenceAleTransport assembles directly on each simplex with barycentric basis
lambda_i. The consistent endpoint mass is rho*V*(1+delta_ij)/20. For an integrated
outward relative face flux q_f and opposite reference vertex xi_f,
  V * J^-1 * (w-grid) = sum_f (q_f/3)*(xi-xi_f).
Thus weak volume and face terms use the SAME owned face fluxes with no inverse
thin physical Jacobian applied to a nearly cancelling velocity subtraction.
Velocity retains all four vector nodal values, not a cell/bay constant.
Nonnegative upwind and temporal losses give the same checked physical energy
identity. The formerly failing case passes GCL5.32455e-16, constant residual
3.10599e-16 and energy identity4.44089e-16. Newborn-old-mass poisoning yields
bit-identical reconstructed fields. Analytic cube P/L/E and translated affine
physical fields are checked independently in two frames. Arbitrary prescribed-
advector angular conservation is NOT asserted by this reference.
This is a different, explicit time/space representation; do NOT promise identical
finite-step trajectories to the previous physical-coordinate trial. It does not
repair a failed matrix, impose an energy cap, change physical cadence or expand
the solver iteration budget.
