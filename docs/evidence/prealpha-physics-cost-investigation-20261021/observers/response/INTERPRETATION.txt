   contact graph, separating stable identity/adjacency/allocated storage from
   event-time geometric validity and event impulse state. Keep generation and
   exact feature ownership; refresh/invalidate geometry from provable bounds or
   actual feature checks, not prior existence. Use endpoint/row dirty revisions
   and deterministic ordered worklists so already-certified clean rows avoid
   repeated kernels, while ALL genuinely affected constraints propagate. Track
   ordinary support-force guesses separately from incremental impact impulses;
   never warm-apply a previous TOI impulse as a resting load. First prove exact

   countless distinct micro-events, explicitly compare physical invariants and
   trajectories rather than claiming bit-identical behavior. This is a justified
   redesign direction, not a completed or guaranteed performance fix.
