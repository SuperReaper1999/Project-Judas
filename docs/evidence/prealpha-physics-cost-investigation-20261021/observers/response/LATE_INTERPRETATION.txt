Source-backed conclusions
-------------------------
1. This is not merely too many arrivals: events per step fall18%, while mean
   event-body domain grows78% and mean event-row domain more than doubles.
   A resting pile's connected support becomes larger as it settles. The engine
   rebuilds almost exclusively existing support, not a corresponding growth in
   genuinely new impact geometry. Current clearing/reconstruction ownership is
   WorldIncrementalImpact.h:19-31,WorldIncrementalImpact.cpp:31-64 and
   ImpactSolver.cpp:160-201. Per-step row work rises68%, manifold work47%.

2. The extra zero-effect row percentage does not offer a whole-solve shortcut:
   40,427,943 of62,277,381 late growing visits leave accumulators unchanged, but
   only551 visits occur after a full proven fixed point. Ongoing coupling keeps
   revisiting rows that were clean earlier. Dependency-indexed scheduling must
   know both endpoint changes and the row's own accumulator state.1,214,022 late
   row visits change accumulators while both represented endpoint velocities
   remain unchanged, so endpoint-dirty tests alone are insufficient. Source:
   ContactSolver.cpp:251-291,305-338;ImpactSolver.cpp:224-237.

3. Warm support continuity deteriorates alongside growing coupling.2,675,706
   nonzero cache entries are zeroed late, averaging2973/step. Only825,274 of
   4,029,644 ordinary rows receive a nonzero warm guess (20.48%). This matches
   the explicit policy atWorldImpactEvents.cpp:625-672, but does NOT isolate how
   much of the next-step cost it causes. Keeping old guesses without a sound
   ordinary-force/impact-impulse model would be an unjustified physics change.
   Discarded cache entries and matched prepared points are not a1:1 cohort.

4. Small deltas remain a numerical description, not a safe propagation cutoff:
   77.49% of1,570,391 late changed-body observations have max mixed component
   delta<=1e-5,53.54%<=1e-7; only4.99% have every component within one ordered ULP.
   WorldImpactEvents.cpp:610-615,684-696 must preserve real changed trajectories
   unless a later architecture supplies conservative validity certificates or
   explicitly changes the integration model. No magnitude cutoff is justified.

5. Confirmed allocation churn scales with reconstructed work:19,541,331 retained
   visited-node inserts and200,044 seen-vector allocations in the late window.
   These are lower-bound storage events, not a measured malloc-time share,
   retained RSS or proof that an allocator replacement solves the architecture.
   Reusing a step/world-owned graph and storage has coherent value, but the
   distinct-pose contact recertification and mechanical propagation remain the
   central engineering problem. Parent's separate native timing control must
   establish actual long-run runtime cost; intrusive observer clocks are not a
   substitute for that measurement.
