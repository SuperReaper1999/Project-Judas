Curved CCD prototype: unrelated filter change destroys physical continuation
=========================================================================

Disposition
-----------
The rotation-world research prototype fails a physical lifecycle invariant.
Disabling collision between an awake actor and an inert static body more than
1 km away changes its motion against its unchanged primary sheet in 3/168
paired trials. One trial newly crosses the original 5 cm actual-sheet gate.
The production baseline has zero physical differences under the same action.
No original test, engine source, fixture, threshold or expected value was
changed for this review. All runs below were executed by the parent agent;
this summary was prepared by reading their raw logs and source only.

Source-level cause and lifecycle interpretation
-----------------------------------------------
Source paths here refer to the frozen rotation-world/sources research copy,
not an adopted production change.

physics/WorldJoints.cpp:153..169, SetPairCollisionEnabled:
  An actual change to actor/inert suppression increments both bodies'
  contactObservationRevision (lines 163..164). It does not distinguish another
  existing physical pair owned by that actor. A no-op returns before the revision
  increments (lines 157..158), consistent with the zero-change noop matrices.

physics/WorldInternal.h:486..487 and 520..526:
  Mesh episode records store revisionA/revisionB. Episode() requires them to
  equal the corresponding bodies' contactObservationRevision, alongside actual
  body generations, primitive identity and mesh asset identity. Changing an
  unrelated pair therefore makes the actor/sheet episode inaccessible.

physics/WorldInternal.h:553..558 and 611..621:
  EpisodeForTime returns no historical selection if Episode() is invalid.
  BeginMeshEpisodes then calls MeshContactFeatures with that null history.
  At the three failed boundaries the ordinary native manifold is already empty;
  the source's front-side rules cannot reconstruct the previous physical
  ownership from that same pose. Empty feature results erase the episode.
  The next real integration therefore uses a different accepted-contact set.

The raw logs demonstrate the required distinction: 156 candidate observation
changes occur, but only three become physical failures. Baseline has 109
observation changes with zero physical failures. Observation invalidation by
itself is permitted by this test; subsequent pose/velocity/response divergence
against the unchanged sheet establishes the defect.

The source mistake is using a body-wide observation publication revision as
the lifetime of pair-specific causal physical state. A future correction needs
an explicit ownership lifetime for the actual physical pair/resource/trajectory.
Simply ignoring all revisions would also be unjustified: reset/teleport, real
primary filter changes, body reuse, and geometry replacement still need valid
invalidation. These lifecycle cases and cold persistence remain distinct
requirements; this test does not claim they were implemented or validated.

Independent lifecycle review limits
-----------------------------------
The research record also checks generations and a raw CollisionAsset pointer,
and publishes accepted feature sets after actual boundaries. Those checks do
not repair the unrelated observation-revision dependency. This adversary proves
one ordinary same-process lifecycle defect; it does not establish a complete
replacement invalidation design, cold-process restoration, atomic failed-step
publication, all filter/reentry semantics, or resource replacement safety.

The production baseline's 69 through-gate diagnostic endpoints remain known
original CCD failures. Its passing unrelated-filter invariant is not a claim
that baseline curved CCD is correct. Conversely, the prototype's original
372/193/193 passes do not override this new three-case physical failure.
