Frozen-friction Schur experiment (REJECTED as a practical solver)
----------------------------------------------------------------
A=J_n M^-1 J_n^T uses the original represented physical mass and cached inertia.
b=J_n*v_current - A*lambda_current - bias; same-event total lambda>=0.
Negative delta-lambda removes a provisional impulse from THIS event only.
No historical/cross-event impulse is removed; no adhesion is introduced.
Original inverse-inertia max asymmetry: 1.4305114746e-6.
Random Schur bilinear relative asymmetry: 1.4510802493e-10.
The operator was NOT silently symmetrized or given artificial compliance.
Sampled diagonal matches the matrix-free operator exactly.
First projected active-CG normal solve hits its bounded 64*256 search limit,
takes about 746 ms and is not converged to its diagnostic 1e-8 target.
Frozen normals reduce max closing to .004319 m/s but leave 98 Coulomb violations.
One ORIGINAL full normal/friction sweep returns max closing to .046992 m/s.
Five such alternating passes take 3.79 seconds and still leave .021558 m/s.
This route is not a performance improvement or a correct full-law replacement.
The original failed run is preserved in schur-3470.json. An unused FISTA helper
was subsequently compiled but never run; it is not claimed as a tested solution.

Full-law safeguarded Anderson experiment (NO measured practical win)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The fixed-point map is one ORIGINAL ContactSolver normal+friction sweep.
Unknowns are same-event normal and vector tangent impulses.
Extrapolation uses at most four history differences, projects to unilateral
and Coulomb limits, and is accepted only when the full-map residual improves
over an ordinary next sweep and the original physical energy budget passes.
Velocities are reconstructed from the event's original state and the original
mass/inertia operator; binary64 accumulation changes rounding relative to
incremental binary32 trial updates, explicitly a diagnostic numerical change.
At most 40 full map evaluations after the original PGS10 initialization:
15 accepted extrapolations, 4 rejected, zero energy-rejected trials.
Max closing .0533478 -> .00376978 m/s; normal KKT L2 .357979 -> .0212713.
Total diagnostic time: 24.77 ms, including state reconstruction and observations.
Original PGS100 takes 23.38 ms and reaches BETTER .00237434 / .00935895 residual.
Thus this implementation is not a demonstrated speedup at matched quality.
No whole-world test or engine adoption is justified by this isolated result.
