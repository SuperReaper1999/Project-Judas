THE DELIVERABLE
An explicitly configured physical character can maintain standing balance, take
corrective steps when an in-place correction is insufficient, genuinely lose its
balance, and attempt a physically driven return to stable standing when the game
allows it and the situation is within the documented support envelope.

This is not an animation studio, universal locomotion brain, or a claim to match
proprietary Euphoria. Judas supplies reusable physical-character control. Project
JavaScript decides whether an actor tries, gives up, dies, gets back up, or resumes
its ordinary motor-driven gameplay.

Core ownership:
GAME INTENT + ACTUAL PHYSICAL STATE -> BALANCE / STEP / RECOVERY REFERENCE
-> BOUNDED ACTUATOR EFFORT -> EXISTING PHYSICSWORLD CONTACTS AND CONSTRAINTS
-> ACTUAL PHYSICAL POSE -> EXISTING FINAL POSE / SOCKETS / SKINNING

A requested pose is not an achieved pose. A selected foothold is not a contact.
A completed reference trajectory is not proof that a character has stood up.

## 2. Required core and bounded first-version envelope

All three are required in the same candidate:
A. Contact-aware physical balance, including recovery from bounded disturbances
   without moving the feet when physically achievable.
B. Actual corrective foot repositioning that extends the demonstrated recovery
   envelope, with touchdown established by real contact.
C. A game-requested, physically driven get-up/recovery attempt from supported fallen
   configurations, ending in stable physical standing before optional motor return.

An initial explicitly mapped, two-legged articulated controller is sufficient.
Its assumptions must be structural and authored, not based on humanoid bone names,
asset filenames or Lastlight/Skate states. Do not claim arbitrary anatomy support.
Use configurable support effectors, body groups, frames and physical limits so
compatible differently named/proportioned rigs can share the implementation.

Minimum successful envelope: ordinary solid ground, modest uneven/slope geometry,
locally coherent nonzero gravity, and modest translating/rotating kinematic supports.
Demonstrate a locally curved radial-gravity case as well as a rigidly rotated setup.
Define actual tested speeds, slopes, gravity variation, rig masses and actuator limits.
A new field of locomotion research is not required for every combination.

Dynamic supports must receive normal physical reactions. Stable balance on every
lightweight rocking object is not promised. Conflicting independently moving supports,
large gravity discontinuities, tiny footholds, zero gravity, missing ground, severe
impacts and obstructed recovery must degrade or refuse honestly, not acquire hidden
assistance. The runtime should explain the observed reason where it can.

Full procedural walking/running/gait generation is not required. Corrective steps
are a bounded response to a balance task, not navigation to arbitrary destinations.
No motion matching, reinforcement-learning pipeline, facial/hand mocap, parkour,
skate trick recognition, injury scoring, combat AI, rope climbing or flying recovery.

## 3. Authority: motor support remains valid, physical balance stays physical

Preserve existing independent uses:
- Animation and M70 IK without a ragdoll remain valid and cheap.
- CharacterMotor locomotion with selected partial physical limbs remains valid.
- Existing M70 active/passive/animation modes and legacy enter/leave keep their

- Configuring M77 does not silently activate it or take locomotion authority.

The physical-standing proof must use a fully dynamic, unpinned articulation,
including its root, after an explicit motor handoff. A capsule standing underneath
it, a kinematic pelvis, invisible support, or a position servo applied directly to
the owner cannot count as physical balance. Ordinary motor-driven games are not
forced to adopt full dynamic locomotion merely because this feature exists.

Use the existing explicit authority transition, or a narrowly extended equivalent,
to enter the physical task. Return to motor/animation only at a safe boundary after
a separate game-authorized request. Resume placement and inherited motion must be
derived from current authoritative physical state and pass existing collision
checks. Refusal must leave a coherent physical actor, not half-retired bodies.

Do not retire a fallen ragdoll, snap the capsule to a clear spot and blend a standing
clip, then call that physical recovery. That existing visual handoff can follow an
actual get-up; it does not substitute for one. Document velocity inheritance when
switching representation. Do not claim total momentum conservation when replacing
an articulated physical model with a query-only CharacterMotor.

Keep one owner per physical degree of freedom and one explicit reference/actuator
composition rule. M77 must not race a game-authored ikTargets batch or add duplicate

controller owns while opted in, how base clip/layer references contribute, and how

## 4. Actual support and articulated-state observations

Build controller observations from a coherent authoritative fixed sample:
- Physical body COM positions, orientations, actual linear/angular velocities,
  masses and inertias. Sum each physical body once; do not count render bones,
  helper joints or the collider-free owner as additional mass.
- Whole-articulation centre of mass and its actual velocity; retain enough angular
  motion information for meaningful disturbance/recovery decisions.
- Actual external contacts for configured feet and, during recovery, other declared
  support regions such as hands/knees. Preserve real filtering and self-collision.
- Contact positions/normals, support identity and point velocity. State explicitly
  whether load/impulse observations are available or approximated.
- Current effective gravity sampled through the existing consumers and the physical
  rig's configuration, not inferred from a camera, animation up or a floor tag.

Trace whether existing contact callbacks contain enough information. A coalesced
public body-pair event is not automatically a full contact manifold. Add a bounded
native observation/snapshot mechanism if necessary without changing existing event
meanings or flooding scripts with contact internals. Public contact subscription
must not be required merely for the native controller to observe its own state.

Distinguish actual supporting contacts from nearby ray hits, remembered contacts,
possible footholds and planted reference targets. A ray finding the floor below an
airborne foot does not grant support. Account for enabled/sensor/layer state and
lost generation-safe handles. Reference/support loss must invalidate intent promptly.

Support-region/centre-of-mass projections are permitted diagnostics or planning
approximations; they are not a universal stability proof. Use velocity and the
available physical actuation, not only whether a static point is inside a polygon.
State the approximations for noncoplanar contacts, moving supports and varying
gravity. Do not infer a semantic floor or an infinite plane behind finite geometry.

Use explicit sole/palm contact frames, shape extents and relevant support patches.
Imported ankle/wrist origins are not automatically the actual contacting surfaces.
Do not fabricate a large support footprint from the collider-free owner's bounds.

## 5. Standing balance and bounded physical control

Implement a documented feedback mechanism that changes coordinated body references
and/or joint actuator effort using observed balance error, motion and support.
Select the mathematical approach after the preflight; the deliverable is not tied
to a branded algorithm. State equations, frames, units, physical assumptions,
saturation handling and stability limitations clearly enough to audit in M78.

Effort reaches the actor through real bounded joint/actuator operations. Existing
M70 implicit drives may be reused/extended; a feed-forward or inverse-dynamics
contribution must have defined joint ownership, units and limits. If a planner uses
virtual forces, explain how they become legitimate joint effort and reactions.
There must be no unpaired external stabilizing force/torque at the root/COM and no
invisible world anchor. Dynamic-to-dynamic actuator reactions remain paired.

Check the actual physical joint axes, drive authority and limits. A limit imposed
only on an IK reference is not proof that the simulated articulation has that
constraint. Where the controller requires a missing generic drive/limit, implement
and validate it through the ordinary joint path, or diagnose the unsupported
configuration explicitly. Do not loosen existing authored limits to pass the lab.

No dynamic-body transform assignment, velocity overwrite, artificial upward thrust,
extra gravity used to glue feet, or post-physics IK that erases the physical response.
Finite actuator caps and documented numerical safety bounds are appropriate; clamping
actual body motion to conceal a failed controller is not. Preserve normal friction,
restitution, mass, inertia, gravity, collision and solver cadence.

In the physical lab, visual physics contribution is full-strength: do not use
poseWeight below one to make an unstable collider rig look upright. Ordinary game
presentation blending remains available under its existing approximation contract.
Keep target pose, actual body pose and displayed pose distinguishable in diagnostics.

Standing must survive a modest real disturbance and settle, not merely stay upright
when initialized at exact rest. There must also be disturbances the same actor cannot
recover from. It must slip or fall when grip/effort/support is inadequate. No scene
or incoming test-button branch selects a prerecorded 'successful stumble'.

Do not defeat ordinary sleep by rewriting identical targets or renormalizing the
same represented quaternion forever. A sleeping stable rig may retain bounded
residual error; meaningful target, support or physical changes must wake correctly.

## 6. Corrective stepping, not animated foot sliding

Use observed motion and remaining support to decide whether a corrective step is
needed, which configured foot can move, and a bounded reachable placement. The game
may allow/deny stepping and set its envelope; the controller chooses mechanics
within that envelope. Do not require the game to implement the native balance
algorithm again in JS.

Candidate generation and clearance use ordinary geometry queries with explicit
filters, ignored self bodies and bounded counts/ranges. Retain selected footholds
in the supporting body's local frame when applicable and validate their lifetime.
Reject unreachable, blocked, overly steep or unsupported placements. Respect real
joint/limb reach, physical collision shapes and the currently loaded world.

A selected foot must actually unload/lift, travel under bounded physical actuation,
and contact the selected or another accepted physical surface. Do not teleport its
body, turn it kinematic, disable collisions for the swing, move the entire entity,
or weld the stance foot to its target. Targets may be trajectories; actual contact
and progress determine touchdown and transfer of support. Timing alone is not proof.

Allow a bounded number of corrective steps/replans per task episode with defined
completion, no-progress and failure criteria. Small corrective sequences are enough;
infinite alternating foot commands that never recover are not a successful result.
The controller must not jitter-select a different support every step without reason.

Measure stance slip, swing-foot clearance/contact, physical support change and
subsequent settling. A blocked swing or disappearing foothold must trigger a real
replan/failure response, not a fake landing. Do not promise exact rotational CCD for
a public cast that only translates; rely on the existing physical sweep/contact
path and document the planner's clearance approximation.

The decisive comparison: from equivalent initial physical state and the same real
perturbation, in-place balance alone fails a chosen case, while enabling stepping
makes at least one actual corrective landing and achieves stable standing.

## 7. Falling, bracing and recovery to standing

Expose a game-controlled recovery task. The game decides whether recovery is
allowed and may choose an authored reference/profile or clip; the engine provides
bounded physical execution, progress and failure diagnostics. No automatic revive,
health test, zombie state, animation-name convention or mandatory get-up policy.

A practical first version may use mapped staged target references and ordinary
existing clips as guides. Reusable profile data can describe support regions,
reference poses and progression conditions. A few original lab reference poses
are acceptable; building an animation authoring application or producing commercial
character animations is not. Do not encode one fixture's joint arrays in native code.

Demonstrate recovery from BOTH a settled face-up and a settled face-down position
on clear supported ground, with modest start-pose variation. The actor must obtain
real supports, raise its real mass through joint effort/contact reactions and reach
stable physical standing. Hand/knee support is allowed when declared and simulated.
The recovery need not solve every tangled, inverted, trapped or airborne ragdoll.

Where useful, a bounded bracing response may precede a fall or failed step. It must
be driven by actual disturbance state, not an impact-animation trigger pretending
to be dynamics. Failure to brace or get up remains possible.

Recovery stage advancement checks actual progress/support, finite effort and
clearance. Reference trajectories can have durations, but a timer expiring cannot
mark recovery complete through a low ceiling or a trapped limb. Include bounded
stall/timeout/attempt limits with reasons. A failed or cancelled task leaves the
current physical pose/velocities intact and transitions to the configured allowed
physical state; it does not replay the initial fall or silently relocate the actor.

If balance or stepping becomes infeasible, report that state and follow only an
explicit configured release/continue policy. A passive request or explicit disable
must stop M77 actuation; old intents must not restore active effort next frame.
Active-to-passive release must preserve current articulation and velocities without
an extra activation impulse. Recovery from passive is another explicit request.

After standing, hold it long enough to prove support before reporting success.
Then demonstrate an optional collision-checked return to the ordinary animated
CharacterMotor and existing crossfades/sockets. A moving support must not add its
velocity twice or reset inherited world motion. Also prove a requested return can
be refused safely when capsule placement is obstructed.

## 8. Gravity, reference frames and moving supports

Never introduce universal world Y, a world-horizontal threshold or a camera-based
balance frame. Configuration uses declared model/contact frames; the controller
uses actual local physical state and gravity/support observations.

Per-entity gravity selection currently does NOT implicitly propagate from an owner
to every child or ragdoll bone. Verify the current tree and preserve that contract.
Sampling only the owner while its physical bones fall under a different field is
not correct. Use the bones' actual gravity; give the lab explicit per-body selections
where necessary. Any added convenience must be opt-in, narrowly owned and restore
prior state safely, not redefine all existing articulation gravity inheritance.

For locally coherent gravity, use the physical rig's sampled accelerations and a
documented local planning frame. In zero/near-zero gravity or strongly conflicting
body fields, report unsupported/unavailable standing support or a defined passive
fallback. No fake down vector, square-root singularity, NaN, or root push to invent
a floor. In-flight internal pose control may remain, but is not standing recovery.

Support motion must use the actual sampled body trajectory and point velocity,
including angular motion about its physical COM. Store contact plans relative to
the specific support, not a stale world position. Any support acceleration estimate
must use real history and time; first activation, reload or teleport cannot fabricate
an enormous derivative. Discontinuities invalidate/replan as appropriate.

A fully dynamic articulation receives support carry through ordinary contact/friction,
not CharacterMotor carry plus a second velocity/transform update. Kinematic supports
are prescribed and may do external work; dynamic supports receive ordinary reactions.
Do not make a dynamic platform infinite-mass merely because the balance task uses it.

Demonstrate translating and gently rotating supports, support removal, an oblique
gravity equivalent, and a modest local patch under RadicalGravity. These validate
frames and compatibility, not arbitrary spinning spaceships, relativistic frames,
whole-planet gait planning or live origin rebasing. Fixed double-origin/local-float
rules remain unchanged. Use transformed-equivalent fixtures to catch axis assumptions.

## 9. Fixed-step integration, API and configuration ownership

Integrate at existing fixed boundaries with a clear phase table. A suitable shape is:
1. JS issues validated task/configuration/mode intent in fixedUpdate.
2. The controller observes the preceding completed physical sample and current
   reference inputs, derives bounded balance/step/recovery intent, and prepares
   the reference/actuator work for this physics interval.
3. Existing PhysicsWorld integrates once with ordinary contacts and constraints.
4. Completed physical state publishes observations/task progress and feeds M47's
   final pose; existing events follow their documented boundary.
5. Presentation interpolates the published result for cameras/sockets/skinning.

Adapt that arrangement to the actual scheduler, documenting any necessary opt-in
extension. Do not make an earlier callback observe a later solve from the same
step, recurse into physics from a signal, or run the controller per camera/render
frame. Pause, zero-step frames and multiple catch-up steps retain normal meaning.

Add the minimum declarative authoring/JS surface necessary to configure, start,
cancel/disable and inspect the task. API names must follow the actual architecture;
do not invent a public signature merely to satisfy this prose. One facade on the
existing articulated owner or a small owned component is sufficient.

Required semantics:
- Explicit stable-key rig/body/support mapping; validated masses/frames/shape
  readiness and required physical degrees of freedom.
- Authored rest/reference posture and optional recovery references; meaningful
  effort, reach, response and attempt limits with documented units.
- Balance/step/recovery permissions, explicit task intent and cancellation.
- Detached current status: active authority/task, readiness/reason, actual COM and
  velocity, actual support identities/points, planned step and actual touchdown,
  recovery progress, saturation and failure/settling observations as applicable.
- Clear distinction between 'requested', 'accepted', 'executing', 'succeeded' and
  'failed/cancelled'. A bounded heuristic must not claim universal recoverability.
- Fixed-boundary mutation with all-or-nothing validation. Bad input or stale owners
  preserve prior valid settings. Loading/unready rigs never count as successful work.

Reuse M70's existing vector/tuple conventions where extending those configurations;
ordinary live Vec3/Quat readbacks keep their existing object shape. Types, examples
and error paths must agree. Avoid a gratuitous mix of interchangeable formats.

If notifications are useful, use existing appropriate lifecycle/event infrastructure
or expose task status for game JS to publish through M73 signals. No second event
bus, callbacks inside solver loops or persistence of transient signal queues.

Provide practical consumer examples: motor-controlled actor temporarily entering
physical balance/recovery and returning; physical actor remaining active after
standing; game refusing get-up and releasing to passive. No modifications to Lastlight
or Claude's game are required or authorized by these examples.

## 12. Proof that can distinguish a controller from a staged animation

Set acceptance metrics/tolerances and a small reproducible perturbation matrix
before judging final results. Report units, masses, friction, drive limits, starting
poses, contact geometry, duration and seed where applicable. No 'passed 200 tests'
without the behaviours and negative controls those tests establish.

Required focused evidence:
1. In-place balance: a free-root rig rejects bounded disturbances from more than
   one direction and settles on real feet. Disturbed trials must include motion,
   not just an exact-rest equilibrium. Effort-off/control-off ablation loses the
   claimed recovery benefit in a documented nontrivial case.
2. Corrective stepping: equivalent setup with stepping denied versus allowed shows
   why a step matters. Observe physical lift, path/contact, support change and
   subsequent stability, not only a planned foot coordinate or 'step count'.
3. Genuine failure: excessive perturbation, no support, inadequate friction/effort,
   unreachable foothold and blocked step cannot all magically succeed. Report real
   outcomes and stable finite failure handling without swallowing diagnostics.
4. Physical recovery: face-up and face-down trials, plus modest pose variations,
   get to stable physical standing; an obstruction and an effort-disabled trial
   do not complete merely because a reference clock reaches its end. Interrupt
   recovery with a push/cancel and show coherent continuing physics.
5. Actuation audit: trace every M77 physical write. Free-flight/gravity-off controls
   with no external contact check that internal actuation does not manufacture net
   linear motion or unpaired whole-system angular impulse. Account for actual
   external gravity/contact/kinematic work rather than demanding energy conservation
   from powered motors. Document numerical tolerance and measured residuals.
6. Frames/supports: rotated-equivalent comparison, modest radial gravity, translated
   and rotating supports, moving-point velocity, dynamic-support reactions and
   support disappearance. Zero gravity gives a defined finite non-standing result.
7. Rig/ownership: both mapped rigs and independent instances; physical root remains
   dynamic, binds/weights unchanged; old motor/partial/IK-only paths still operate.
8. Lifecycle: actual QuickJS requests, invalid batches/stale handles, pause and
   catch-up timing, save in flight with fresh-process restoration, reload, normal
   Play/Stop and region pins/revisit with no stale reservations or duplicate task.

Use state/physical measurements independent of controller success flags. Where
feasibility estimates are approximate, label them as estimates. Support-load or
contact probes must observe the ordinary solver, not return an expected fixture
answer. All asserted successes should identify the actual test envelope.

The stand/step proof must not quietly use hidden torso forces, foot anchors,
extra friction, body teleports, collision suppression, animated root translation,
physical-pose blending or a helper floor. Disabled pose drives as a negative control
must include every relevant M77 effort contribution, not leave a hidden drive active.

Analytic/contact/math unit checks are useful but not enough. Exercise Application,
actual JS VM, renderer, physics, normal resource readiness, saves, prefabs and exports.
Use the actual scene-input harness; correct fixture mistakes honestly and retain
real failures. Do not construct synthetic failure evidence after the fact.

## 13. Performance and implementation cadence

This is substantial work, not a mandate to run all historical tests after each edit.
Use a compact implementation plan: observations/actuation, balance/steps, recovery,
then integration and the planned affected Release validation. Necessary small
math/controller experiments and fault reproductions are allowed while developing.
Do not turn the plan into serial approval gates or deliver a diagnostics-only half
milestone instead of the three required capabilities.

Implement the coherent change, then perform one planned affected Release build and
focused verification pass. Genuine failures permit necessary corrections and affected
reruns. This is not a one-attempt trap. After repeated failure of the same approach,
surface the mechanism/constraint and a bounded change of plan rather than tuning
magic numbers indefinitely or weakening acceptance without discussion.

Profile zero-use, existing M70 baseline, one active actor and a modest four-actor
load using the same declared rig/scene where comparisons are claimed. Separate
controller/queries, reference IK, drives, contacts/CCD, pose publication and rendering.
Record median/p95/maximum, first activation, landing/first-contact, stepping, get-up,
settled state, memory/work bounds and solver saturation. A standing-versus-collapsed
contact workload is not a clean same-workload speedup comparison.

Use real wall-clock application runs for stalls; fixed scripted frames completing
eventually do not prove responsive playback. Preserve outliers and cold/steady scope.
Do not time under concurrent VM compilation, CPU/GPU saturation or screenshot encoding
and present that as isolated controller cost. Capture overhead can be measured and
labelled separately. Do not claim a universal FPS guarantee or exact allocator census.

Bound support candidates, query radius, mapped joints/contacts, solver iterations,
step candidates, recovery attempts/history and allocations. Reuse validated per-instance
mappings/scratch buffers. No whole-scene scan per foot, reimport per step or uncontrolled
per-contact heap churn. A cap must reject/defer with a reason, not silently drop mass,
contacts or simulation quality.

Preserve the existing quaternion/contact-history corrections, M75 exact-range BVH
path and ordinary sleeping. Do not disable CCD, skip collision pairs, reduce physical
cadence, force sleep, lower mesh quality or use elapsed-time heuristics to conceal a
freeze. A discovered pathological stall/regression needs a current-tree reproduction
and cause; do not assume an old consumer report proves the current bug. Consult if
a necessary remedy would breach protected scope. Do not hand over a visually upright
lab that repeatedly locks the application for seconds without flagging that blocker.
