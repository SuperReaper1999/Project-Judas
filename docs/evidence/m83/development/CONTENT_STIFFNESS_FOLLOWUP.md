# Initial lab material authoring failure

`application-content-first.txt` preserves the first executable coupled lab
probe after the separate missing-yaw scene-format correction. `content-first/`
contains its exact generator, flat scene, project controller, image and hashes.
The solver, 60 N sustained load, 450 N overload and check thresholds were unchanged.

The initial orange material copied M80's 0.0005 shear compliance into a much
slimmer 1.2 × 0.26 × 0.36 m suspension member. Under the ordinary bearing load its
plastic rest change reached 0.143402 m and its finite 180 N mount released before
the intended overload phase. The now-free carrier travelled 3.75773 m. This
invalidated the lab's intended still-connected plastic-drive comparison; it was
not an engine continuation or collision failure.

The current authored orange shear compliance is 0.00005. Substeps, iterations,
plasticity, mount strengths, physical inputs and checks are unchanged. The same
probe then measured 0.0625187 m retained plastic-rest change, 0.300917 m carrier
displacement and 0.998050 axis alignment while still transmitting real drive.
The intended overload and ordinary ram contact subsequently released that mount.
The stronger control stayed connected. This is content stiffness selection for
the actual section, not a solver correction or a hidden damage policy.

Probe timings taken alongside builds or software rendering are diagnostic;
the final performance result is measured separately on an idle Linux host.
