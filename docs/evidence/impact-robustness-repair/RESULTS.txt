Mechanisms and correction
-------------------------
1. A light anisotropic box spun up by off-centre impacts can leave the original
12-iteration angular Newton solve outside its convergence basin. Retained
Claude's interval-continuation fallback, which solves the SAME midpoint equation
m_mid - m0 - (dt/2) cross(m_mid, I^-1 m_mid) = 0. It activates only after the
original solve fails, follows roots from zero interval, and retains explicit
failure if bounded continuation cannot converge. It does not clamp angular
velocity, substitute an unconserved predictor, change the physics timestep, or
simulate hidden substeps. Momentum norm and kinetic energy follow from dotting
the midpoint equation with m_mid and I^-1 m_mid respectively. The committed
fixture checks the equation's residual as well as both invariants after float
rounding. The original successful Newton path remains unchanged in 20,000
independent sampled inputs (bit-exact returned generators).

2. Touching pairs were excluded from continuous impact searches even when spin
could sweep another corner through the support during the remaining step.
Retained Claude's generic spinning-support classification and penetration-target
search. Sweep threshold is the existing primitive HALF-dimension feature (half
full box thickness); target increase is .25 of that feature (one eighth full
box thickness). Target is relative to actual starting penetration, not a global
rest-pose reset. Existing bounded temporal sampling and impact budgets remain;
spinning pairs still count as supports for inelastic restitution. No change to
controller settings, mesh, mass, cadence, CCD quality or gameplay APIs.

3. In deep actual overlap, the mesh SAT response normal can oppose the selected
triangle front and push a thin body farther through. Retained the front-normal
correction ONLY AFTER SAT separation rejection and ONLY for actual overlap
(s.gap <= 0). Separated speculative contacts retain their separation and normal.
PR #14 replaced the SAT result before rejecting separated finite triangles,
creating ghost contacts on the triangle's infinite plane. This correction keeps
finite edge separation, contact margins, one-sided culling and two-sided reversal
intact. Both full manifolds and Boolean existence use this same corrected path.

Performance and limitations
---------------------------
See performance.json for all six identical 300-step / 60 Hz / 1.2 m/s stress
fixtures, measured sequentially with deterministic repeats. This is one timing
sample per scenario, not a general game-FPS certification. Combined reported
scenario time 7,789.58 -> 9,660.07 ms. Costs are mixed: dense flat sheet
1,561.13 -> 1,668.25 ms; dense curved trough 1,607.19 -> 4,564.88 ms, worst step
79.32 -> 82.60 ms, with more impact events. This cost is NOT hidden or claimed
as a universal speed improvement. Ragdoll floor maximum penetration improves
41.96 -> 14.86 mm, flat sheet 29.38 -> 11.32 mm (6x6) and 24.58 -> 17.58 mm
(60x60). Dense flat-sheet fast-box crossings 3/24 -> 0/24.

Curved-trough fast-box tunnelling remains 24/24 in this corpus: concave-mesh
conservative advancement and centroid-based one-sided culling are separate known
limitations, not repaired or certified here. Trajectories/contact timing change;
only the ordinary successful angular solve is claimed bit-exact. Bounded
sampling and convergence limits still exist. FLU-001, old-save limitations and
incomplete M78 coverage remain unchanged. No fluid code/research changes.
