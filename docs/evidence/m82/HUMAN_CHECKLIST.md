# M82 human review — accepted

Open `projects/powertrain_lab/powertrain_lab.judasproj` or its ordinary exported
package. The operator accepted the Linux desktop watch demonstration on
2026-10-13 and authorized Stable promotion; see [HUMAN_VALIDATION.md](HUMAN_VALIDATION.md).
This records the operator's overall acceptance without inventing individual
check outcomes. The checklist remains available for future review.

1. Drive the orange buggy with W/S and A/D; confirm tyre contact moves its chassis.
2. Open the clutch with F, hold Space and apply throttle; observe engine RPM
   rising while the wheels remain braked. Release Space and re-engage the clutch
   to observe actual load response and acceleration.
3. Use Q/E to change gear; compare RPM/vehicle speed and clutch slip.
4. Brake with Space; confirm the vehicle slows physically.
5. Add/remove the 225 kg payload with B and reduce/restore grip with H; compare
   suspension, wheel slip and engine/differential load.
6. Select the cyan electric rover with Tab and compare its six independent motors.
7. Select the purple shaft machine with Tab. Apply throttle, use B for brake load,
   and Space to stop its physical flywheel. Check there is no scripted RPM display.
8. Use 2 for oblique uniform gravity and 3 for real radial gravity; drive/brake
   without a global-up assumption.
9. Use P to spawn a complete independent mechanical prefab and Tab to select it.
10. Request/release the garage with F3 and check revisit, pause, reload and Stop
    leave no stale mechanical state.
11. Save with F5, load with F8 and verify commands/rotor state and physical response.
12. Press V to watch one project-JS tour, then take control again. The deck is
    finite; manual steering/braking is needed for prolonged driving.
13. Confirm the moved exported standalone behaves equivalently.

Stable promotion was explicitly authorized for the exact tested candidate.
Windows validation is informational and remains separate from this Linux review.
