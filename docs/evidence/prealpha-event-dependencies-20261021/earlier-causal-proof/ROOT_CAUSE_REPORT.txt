PROVEN CAUSAL CHAIN
1. RigidMotionSegment::EvaluateGeometry evaluates elapsed time, positions and
   quaternions in binary32 (src/RigidMotion.h:28-53). At tiny time increments,
   represented geometry advances in coordinate-sized jumps.
2. The contact predicates classify that represented geometry. Near tangency,
   these jumps can create separation/contact transitions that do not exist in
   the underlying continuous anchored trajectory.
3. WorldCCDSearch::ImpactTime (src/physics/WorldCCDSearch.cpp:546 onward) can
   select such a transition as an impact. Contact continuation is checked by
   SupportContinuationSafe (same file:448 onward).
4. AdvanceImpacts builds and solves the connected contact island. Changes to
   velocities replace motion segments and invalidate incident pair events
   (src/physics/WorldImpactEvents.cpp:588-658). In this packed scene that means
   hundreds of bodies and thousands of rows/pairs, repeatedly.

IMPORTANT CONTRACT AND SCOPE QUALIFICATIONS
Current engine geometry explicitly follows represented float arithmetic.
Native predicates are correct for their supplied rounded poses. Thus the
continuous model comparison establishes an architectural numerical limitation;
it is not proof that a SAT sign or native query violates its current float-pose
contract. Earlier sub-ULP quaternion renormalization protection already exists;
that is not a missing fix discovered here.
This investigation classifies one representative pair, not all 61 events.
Residual closing velocities and genuine later collisions also exist. Several
certified separated prefixes lead to real contact later in the SAME remaining
step: dropping their pairs or skipping the interval would be wrong.
Coarse diagnostic phase timers include observation overhead. They show repeated
discovery/rescheduling is substantial, but are NOT a production speedup result
or a reliable precise production cost partition. No repaired-runtime FPS is
claimed. Memory bandwidth has not been measured.

REPAIR BOUNDARY
A coherent repair must define continuous collision geometry and contact-episode
ownership consistently across TOI, manifold generation, solver publication and
motion replacement, while preserving the externally exposed float state where
required. Merely computing one predicate in double can introduce inconsistent
geometry elsewhere. A previous broad double experiment already failed physical
parity and performance; it is not an accepted solution.
Required controls for a future repair: native replay, near-tangent episode
continuity, real separation/re-impact, future collisions after a safe prefix,
curved/finite-feature CCD, physical invariants and matched full-pile timing.
Do not add event caps, reduce cadence/quality, inflate contact tolerances,
ignore small velocity changes or return stale cached TOIs to force performance.
Parallel execution may accelerate independent work, but cannot by itself remove
this causal chain of sequential event/solve/replan dependencies.
