ROOT CAUSE AND CORRECTION
Old transport deposits equal/opposite linear momentum into cell means at
different centroids, but does not evolve their local angular moments. Orbital
angular momentum changes by the centroid displacement cross transported impulse.
That is missing state evolution, not insufficient pressure iterations.

Evolve affine velocity moments with the weak conservative flux:
 b_i(v) = integral_K grad(v):(u tensor u) dV
          - integral_boundaryK v dot u_upwind q dA.
u is the existing affine L2 velocity projection; q remains the actual shared
primary P1 normal flux, not the potentially different normal projection of u.
The symmetric interior stress has zero contraction against rigid-rotation tests.
A shared face transfers momentum at the same actual point for both owners, with
both local angular and orbital first moments. No corrective spin is added later.

For divergence-free states, the existing Green identity and polynomial projection
imply dE/dt = -sum_faces integral |q|*|jump(u)|^2/2 <=0. A backward-Euler prototype
solves pressure INSIDE each nonlinear iterate at fixed1/240s. It verifies
 E_new-E_old + ||d_new-d_old||_M^2/2 + dt*D_upwind =0,
using the existing positive kinetic factors for energy and convergence norms.
The maximum16-iteration policy is fixed; failed iteration rejects without
publication. Tests actually fail with a one-iteration budget. No substeps,
damping, artificial inertia, energy clamp, quantity repair or threshold changes.
