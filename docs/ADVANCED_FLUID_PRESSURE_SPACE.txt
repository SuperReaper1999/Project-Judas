NEW BOUNDED PRESSURE REFERENCE
-------------------------------
CutPotential.h changes the discrete pressure representation rather than trying
another tolerance on the same tiny-fragment constraint system.

- Continuous P1 pressure lives on a bounded 2x2x2 background tetrahedral mesh.
- Actual connected wet pieces, actual exact cut geometry and actual body planes
  supply the volume and solid-boundary integrals. No scalar water level replaces
  this geometry, and no fake particles represent the body.
- Pressure identities are separated by actual wet-component connectivity.
- Fluid velocities here are CELL AVERAGES. This is an incompressible weak-pressure
  reference, NOT a conservative material/face-flux reconstruction.
- Density is1000 kg/m3 in this reference. Open top has explicit zero pressure;
  closed-container gauge handling is explicitly rejected, not silently invented.
- Existing rank-aware native joint reduction supplies actual constrained body
  mobility, rather than assigning infinite mass because an object looks supported.

MATHEMATICAL CONTRACT
----------------------
p is the time-integrated pressure, and phi_i is a pressure basis function.
For each actual wet cell c:
  D_f(i,c) = integral_Omega_c grad(phi_i) dV
For the actual fluid-outward solid normal n and lever arm r:
  D_r(i) = -integral_Gamma_solid phi_i [n, r cross n] dA
With physical fluid/rigid mass matrices M_f and M_r:
  A = D_f M_f^-1 D_f^T + D_r M_r^-1 D_r^T
  A p = D_f u* + D_r V*
  u = u* - M_f^-1 D_f^T p
  V = V* - M_r^-1 D_r^T p
The SAME boundary operator supplies both pressure response and rigid impulse.
For native bilateral joints, substitute the established mass-whitened projected
mobility and constrained predictor, then recover the joint reactions from that
same simultaneous solution. Native still owns rigid integration/history.

The projection work identity is verified independently:
  E_before - E_after = .5 (D u*)^T p
No energy damping, pressure repair, volume clamp or force correction is applied.
Pressure is not constrained nonnegative here; cavitation and moving free surfaces
are not qualified by this single-phase reference.

PERFORMANCE SCOPE
-------------------
The reference uses48 fluid-average cells and18 pressure nodes. Across100 identical
solves, typical pressure median is .0028 ms, p95 .0029 ms and worst below .01 ms.
That excludes geometry preparation. Exact cut geometry costs roughly20–130 ms,
and operator geometry assembly roughly8–100 ms in the recorded cases. These are
small oracle workloads using arbitrary-rational geometry, not production FPS or
a scalable-backend speedup claim. Comparing these numbers with the expensive
per-tet QR is a method comparison with different unknowns, NOT equivalent runtime
feature certification. Geometry caching/scaling and transport still need work.
