MECHANISMS ESTABLISHED

The remaining hitches are not explained by rendering or mouse movement alone.
In the preserved moving-camera capture, an approximately 204 ms frame contained
eight fixed steps totalling approximately 200 ms. A frame's last fixed-step
statistic alone understated that accumulated work.

An event/query trace of the unchanged 1,024-piece scene found:

  Step 153: 138 distinct CCD impacts, 3,194 response rows. Updating local contact
  groups repeatedly scanned 303,999 completed-observation rows and 383,847
  warm-start rows. These are repeated visits, not that many unique contacts.
  Those two stages totalled about 4.7 ms in this instrumented trace.

  Step 461: 13 distinct CCD impacts. Of 12,730 searches, 4,087 terminated at a
  proximity miss; these spent about 11.2 ms of aggregate diagnostic query time.
  Their positive-margin SAT comparisons rebuilt full interval expressions even
  where the existing arithmetic error bound could certify the decision.

These were real sources of avoidable work. The events were not duplicate copies
of one impact. Changed trajectories can move future impact times even by small
amounts; a previous sampled no-impact result is not valid merely because a new
velocity is close. No tolerance-based velocity cutoff or blind reuse was added.
Trace timings are diagnostic, not ordinary-run speed comparisons.

CORRECTIONS

1. ContactGroupUpdates stages only affected primitive groups in the sorted warm
   and observation stores. It preserves generation-sensitive erase/retention,
   bitwise warm guesses, zero-load new identities and stable within-group order.
   Untouched groups are read by binary search, not repeatedly scanned globally.
   One ordered splice publishes on completion or failure unwind. A coupled
   fallback publishes first before its existing global invalidation. This is
   derived step-local mutation bookkeeping, not another persistent history or
   physics model. No solver consumes unpublished history.

2. The existing adaptive SAT filter now certifies the positive-margin polynomial
   from an outward enclosure of the scalar result. Its established scalar-to-
   interval error bound, original interval axis norm and quaternion denominator
   preserve the old interval branch. Unresolved, out-of-domain, non-nearest and
   exact-boundary cases keep the original interval/exact evaluation. It supplies
   no approximate gap, witness, contact normal or response. Near-margin equality
   and adjacent binary32 values have an independent exact-arithmetic oracle.

3. Eligible independent CCD queries borrow the existing bounded job service over
   a frozen event boundary. The CCDQuery view owns private derived samples and
   counters; workers cannot mutate bodies, motion, warm starts or the frontier.
   The owner joins and publishes canonical results before solving the next event.
   Cheap disjoint bounds stay serial. Small batches, compound/mesh/prescribed
   paths, absent/busy/shutdown services and over-budget work have serial paths.
   Limits: four configured lanes by default, 256 heavy-query minimum, 16,384
   request batch maximum, 32 MiB retained sample-payload budget plus metadata.
   These select an execution path; no collision request is discarded.

The solver, ten sweeps, 60 Hz cadence, CCD precision/sampling budgets, scene,
object count, camera controls and catch-up policy are unchanged. No public JS,
component, save-format or physical gameplay interface changed.

PRESERVED ATTEMPTS AND LIMITS

Full-run per-query attribution, exact-step event traces and their raw records
remain available. Naive and cache-aware lazy orientation preparation failed to
produce a reliable gain. Lowering the worker threshold to 32 queries also lost
time with current scratch preparation; it was rejected. The margin-only probe
had no reliable worst-step benefit by itself; the combined final result above
is the actual delivery, not a sum of prototype gains.

A repeated run was correctly rejected by the source guard after this module's
Markdown documentation changed during measurement. Its core/fixture stayed
unchanged; the raw rejected run is retained and a fresh frozen-source retry
supplies the accepted comparison. An initial make invocation discovered a newly
added target only after regeneration; the subsequent explicit target build and
all tests pass. Neither event is disguised as an engine physics failure.

Remaining limit: approximately 191 ms live hitches still occur during the dense
settling burst. At step 461 the final diagnostic still spends about 10.4 ms
summed CCD-query CPU, 7.2 ms growing-contact discovery and 5.2 ms solving. These
are not additive wall-time categories. Correct event-by-event dependencies and
fresh geometry are still required. This work removes two established sources
of waste and safely schedules independent queries; it does not establish that
all remaining search work is necessary or that no further improvement exists.
Further certified exclusion/dependency improvements need their own causal proof.
Do not blame hardware alone or call this universal 60 Hz qualification.

Public physics/JS APIs, save schema and physical policy remain unchanged.
Existing games, protected history, accepted fluids and independent research
were not modified. FLU-001 and unrelated deferred issues remain deferred.
