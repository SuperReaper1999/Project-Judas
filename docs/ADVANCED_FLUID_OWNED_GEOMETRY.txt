WHAT WAS CORRECTED IN THE REFERENCE
-----------------------------------
The previous translating-piston ALE compiler created axis-aligned vertices and
then searched for exact coplanarity with the ACTUAL body. A native quaternion
with z=1.6068929173385094e-11 left zero body rows. This cannot be corrected by
snapping the body, matching planes approximately or ignoring the missing rows.

The new private OwnedCutGeometry prepares the actual solid's six planes, clips
the owned wet regions against them and retains exact homogeneous vertex ancestry.
It subtracts real solid geometry, rather than applying a normal to an unchanged
mesh. Solid normals come from their owning planes. Exact-coordinate differences
are formed before rounding local edges. Physical faces stay attached under the
exact previously failing native quaternion (57 solid-owned faces in this test).

Two further representability issues were reproduced:
1. Cholesky of an explicitly assembled world covariance loses positive pivots in
   valid thin tetrahedra. The equivalent reference-simplex factorization E L_ref
   avoids covariance cancellation; inverse and reference-corner evaluations must
   likewise avoid subtracting rounded nearly coincident world points.
2. Exact expansion products used for the deeper metric calculations can underflow
   even though the final local geometric volume is representable. An independent
   arbitrary-rational metric reference avoids intermediate exponent exhaustion.
   No real cut fragment, mass or thickness is discarded. The private reference
   uses locally extracted Boost 1.90 headers for rational arithmetic. This is NOT
   a new Judas runtime, SDK or distributed dependency, nor a proposed final fast
   implementation. Exact topology remains distinct from rounded physical metrics.

1.13-radian rotations; equivalent contained capacity, actual solid surface area,
balanced normals, divergence-theorem solid volume and exact face-plane ownership.
The partially clipped case asserts ownership, not an invented full-body area.

MotionOwnershipTests: 4417 checks. Sixteen native moving/rotating box snapshots
are compared with an independently stepped native world. Actual poses and linear/

The exact Stage6 first-divergent quaternion is an additional explicit regression.
This observes actual motion; it does NOT yet couple pressure or advect material.

TopologyRemeshTests: 10 checks. Real dry gap gives two connected wet components;
only actual added bridge geometry reconnects them. Preparing that state does not
mutate the old topology. An unchanged affine field is restricted onto a genuinely
different same-time partition (1172 versus1454 tets): quantity error8.88e-16 m3,
momentum error4.73e-12, angular error1.46e-12 and energy error0 in the recorded run.
This is exact field restriction, not moving-boundary flow or arbitrary donor
transfer. It does not prove that uncovered cells can be initialized arbitrarily.

PRESSURE APPROACHES THAT WERE REJECTED
-------------------------------------
Merely repairing geometry and cell metrics does not finish the pressure backend.
With one independent affine fluid state per cut tetrahedron, a nominally tiny
solid rotation creates extremely thin physical/arrangement elements. Correct
metrics remove earlier covariance/NaN failures, but the mass-Gram pressure solve
still fails its unchanged positive-pivot requirement. No diagonal regularization,
additional solver iterations or minimum fragment size was introduced.

A trial groups only exactly connected fragments within each background voxel,
retaining the real surface geometry and all volume. It reduces roughly1000
integration fragments to8 physical cells. Duplicate affine patch equalities are
reduced using three exactly noncollinear owned vertices on each plane. A bounded
column-pivoted QR handles dependent equality rows and independently verifies ALL
physical residuals. It uses a standard machine-epsilon-scaled numerical rank
criterion, not a material cut-off. This is only an equality reference, not a
unilateral-contact, cavitation or production pressure solver.

That trial is ALSO REJECTED: an aligned hydrostatic case reports zero endpoint
speed and residual1.37e-16, yet body impulse is(-.005109375,8.575234375,-8.077921875)
Ns instead of(0,8.175,0). Reversing equivalent constraints retains a calm endpoint
but changes the torque norm from16.5741 to26.8426. Constraint rank88 versus168
rows exposes redundant reactions; the solid wrench is not a trustworthy physical
pressure result. This is a discrete pressure/velocity compatibility defect in
this attempted coarsening, not a new MK2 defect or a permissible force ambiguity.
Do not publish it, repair its force afterward or accept velocity residual alone.
The first diagnostic compared only linear reaction differences and failed to
recognize the torque difference; both that mistake and the corrected wrench
control are retained. Existing hydrostatic or force tolerances were not weakened.
