DECISION
This is a substantial PRIVATE numerical prototype, not an Alpha fluid unlock.
The fine, per-pose cut-tetrahedron velocity construction has been replaced in
this experiment by bounded polyhedral face-flux operators. Construction is much
faster and the named algebra, hydrostatic, native-joint and work checks pass.
However, independent physical comparisons expose substantial coarse added-mass
error, and disconnected pieces within a background seed remain unsupported.
Do not select this as a production fluid backend on the strength of its passing
algebra or timing. Stage14 remains the preserved private reference; neither it
nor this experiment is a completed time-dependent incompressible fluid system.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY IMPLEMENTED
The archived Stage16 sources contain:
- H(div)-inspired k=1 polyhedral kinetic operators: three primary normal-flux
  moments per actual face, plus three interior velocity and three angular
  moments. Dimension is 3F+6, independent of integration triangulation count.
- Explicit polynomial projection and a positive complementary kinetic metric.
  For affine-velocity DOF map N, L2 projection L, polynomial Gram G and positive
  complementary metric S, M=rho L^T G L+(I-NL)^T S(I-NL). The metric is affine
  consistent, not an assertion of exact continuum unresolved-flow energy.
- Four weak continuity equations determine div(v) in P1. Enforcing these makes
  this represented divergence zero. The three linear equations eliminate mean
  velocity moments; the remaining integrated face-flux equation stays explicit.
  Interior angular variables are eliminated by an exact 3x3 Schur complement,
  retaining the eliminated source-energy constant and reaction derivatives.
- Shared primary P1 face fluxes, a sparse positive kinetic factor and a pressure
  Schur factor. Closed components have one pressure gauge removed only after
  verifying compatible prescribed volume flux; open-top components retain full
  pressure rank. Unsupported or nonrepresentable results fail before publication.
- Sequential exact solid subtraction with at most six exterior convex pieces
  per background tetrahedron. Integration cones remain geometric integration
  only. No velocity unknown is allocated per cone or transient tetrahedron.
  Exact inherited-cap adjacency detects separate wet pieces; the current merged
  operator refuses those cases rather than joining disconnected water.
- Stable shared face coordinates selected from exact geometric extrema. Exact
  local differences and dot products precede long-double conversion. Full face
  Gram/mixed integrals use positive simplex integration. Convex hulls select
  coordinates only; actual apertures, including concavity, are integrated.
- Atomic geometry-epoch updates reuse unaffected owned factors. Invalid source,
  geometry, rounding, incompatible sealed flow and unsupported topology paths
  retain the previously published epoch. This is not swept-water evolution.
- A genuine positive-area boundary-touch defect in this prototype was reproduced
  and corrected: an otherwise empty solid/seed intersection still needs its
  wetted boundary footprint. Exact owned-plane polygon clipping preserves the
  background remainder and assigns the solid patch correctly. Five full-epoch
  axis/oblique cases and zero-area edge controls pass; the initial failure stays
  archived. This is a private geometry correction, not a claimed PBF repair.

Primary flux is authoritative for normal transport. The cell's affine velocity
projection is NOT substituted for its boundary trace. q0 is the integrated
normal flux by definition; it is not recomputed through a rounded Gram inverse.
There is no diagonal regularization, damping, dropped small face, volume repair,
iteration-budget increase, reduced cadence or fitted load-response coefficient.

REFERENCE CHECKS THAT PREVENT A FALSE ACCEPTANCE
1. The original rounded-vertex tiny-face oracle fails thirteen checks for BOTH
   the exact and fast collectors, identically at about 1.0571e-8 against 1e-8.
   Preserve that failure. The original tool output was not saved as a raw file;
   the archive includes a final-source reproduction of the unchanged fixture.
   A separate independent factored-coordinate oracle
   computes area vectors and centred coordinates before rounding and reintegrates
   primary flux to about 2.6e-19 without changing the original threshold. This
   does NOT make the rounded-point EvaluateFaceNormal API magically precise.
2. The initial fine comparison mixed a sealed compact tank with Stage14's open
   top. Those initial rows are diagnostic/noncomparable, preserved as such.
   Matching the open-top boundary does NOT remove the large coarse discrepancy.
3. Stage14's fine affine field imposes FOUR MACRO divergence moments, not one
   equation per fine tetrahedron. A new exact per-tetrahedron diagnostic aggregates
   back to the original equations to roundoff, then measures the distinction.
   At oblique prescribed motion, the weak field's maximum local divergence reaches
   208.52/s, while its original macro-volume residual remains tiny. This is a
   weak-Galerkin discretization distinction, not proof that its historical
   conservation tests were wrong or that all weak incompressibility is invalid.
   It is NOT a converged pointwise-incompressible reference by itself.
4. On the SAME fine geometry and boundary conditions, imposing each tetrahedron's
   integrated divergence raises oblique combined-motion energy from .0284251 J
   to .0759415 J; strict maximum divergence is below 6.2e-16/s. The compact
   open-top coarsest model gives .316079 J. The strict finite space has richer
   background subface traces and is an admissible incompressible comparison,
   not an exact continuum answer or an identical-DOF accuracy oracle.
5. An independent continuum-admissible closed-tank trial is constructed as
     u = curl(chi A),
     A = .5 U_COM cross r - .5 |r|^2 omega.
   The separable C1 cubic cutoff chi equals one throughout the body and has value
   AND gradient zero at tank walls. Polynomial-exact quadrature in exact arithmetic,
   evaluated in long double, and
   an independent rational translation formula agree. Translation trial energy
   is .151353137415 J, while the coarsest compact result is .159423109859 J,
   5.33% ABOVE this admissible upper bound. This establishes approximation error
   independently of either numerical pressure reference. The mixed-motion trial
   is looser and inconclusive; it is not counted as physical acceptance.
6. Fixed PHYSICAL domain/body refinement (not simply enlarging the tank) reduces
   compact open-top combined-motion energy from .316079 J at h=1 to .127560 J at
   h=.5. Complementary-metric energy falls from .257591 to .051953 J. Further
   h=1/3 and h=1/4 geometries encounter disconnected-within-seed rejection.
   Two successful resolutions do NOT establish asymptotic convergence. Original
   unsuccessful probes and their exit codes remain evidence, not green tests.

WHAT REMAINS / NEXT TECHNICAL BOUNDARY
Do not tune S to manufacture agreement. The merged seed may be nonconvex/holed;
its complementary kinetic response has no demonstrated uniform accuracy bound
on these cuts. A justified next comparison is to retain the already bounded
convex exterior pieces as physical cells, partition their artificial cap faces
into actual fluid-fluid and fluid-solid ports, then condense their internal
ports. A single normal polynomial across a mixed solid/fluid cap would incorrectly
lock independent ports; explicit common-aperture ownership is required. A true
local div/curl/Neumann energy operator is another principled but larger option.
Neither alternative is implemented or claimed qualified by this checkpoint.

Also outstanding: swept wall geometry/GCL, real displaced-water and momentum
transport, wet/dry/free-surface evolution, multiple solid ownership including
actual floor/planet collision geometry, long-running native contact/CCD, M54
quantity ownership, application persistence/lifecycle and export. Endpoint-only
geometry plus new source callbacks is not a time-dependent fluid simulation.
The original Terrain planet failure is NOT declared fixed.
