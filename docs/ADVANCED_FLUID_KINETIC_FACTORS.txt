WHAT WAS ESTABLISHED

1. Positive local kinetic metrics can become singular after forming a rounded
   global Gram matrix. In the true voxel-corner fixture, independent cycle-flow
   coordinates shared a tiny tree aperture. A saved rounded2x2 principal minor
   is exactly zero even at90-digit analysis. This is loss during assembly, not
   proof that the physical kinetic functional is indefinite.

2. Preserve the defining root instead:
       M = rho L^T G L + (I-NL)^T S (I-NL) = F^T F,
       F = [sqrt(rho) R_G L ; R_S (I-NL)],
   with R_G^T R_G=G and R_S^T R_S=S. QR eliminates angular and free coordinates
   without first forming their rounded normal equations. Loads, body derivatives
   and reconstruction use the same root. A maximum-exact-aperture spanning tree
   improves coordinates while retaining every face and the same admissible space.
   No damping, rank removal, geometric pruning or artificial mass is added.

3. Exact per-cell continuity must survive material transport, not only a rounded
   pressure residual. Whole-connected-cavity incidence parameterization removes
   the n2 local-interface balance failure before minimization. Every original
   physical cell, aperture, integration simplex and owner is retained. This is
   algebraic condensation, not moving water through disconnected regions.

4. Evaluate the same exact incidence equations by a tree recurrence instead of
   a dense symbolic expansion.32 arbitrary primary-coordinate trials produce
   exactly equal internal flows, physical face means and cell/region balances.
   The kinetic solve itself is unchanged.

5. Native added mass can be derived from the same prepared Schur operator rather
   than six artificially imposed unit-flow reconstructions. Final545 checks
   validate actual native fixed/hinged/free-body endpoints and reject omitted
   native reaction before publication. This is one accepted solver boundary,
   not continuous CCD/friction/contact/material coupling.
