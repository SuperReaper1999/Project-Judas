1. THE ACTUAL LOCALITY PROBLEM
The existing conforming cut compiler applies all six solid planes throughout
the background arrangement, including far outside the physical body. Its output
is mathematically valid, but moving a small solid changes artificial distant
subdivisions and therefore the local kinetic bases/factorizations built on them.
A cache wrapped around that representation cannot provide genuine locality.

GeometryMotionTests uses the SAME conforming plane arrangement, a canonical
exact-coordinate pulling order and selected-seed recompilation. It matches a
fresh full rebuild TETRAHEDRON FOR TETRAHEDRON and in exact volume/first moments.
But every nonzero translation/rotation in the two-cell-wide fixture dirties all
48 seeds. Matched geometry+moment costs include update229.28ms versus full274.92ms
for the tiny-angle case, and update170.18ms versus full199.63ms at .07rad.
These slight implementation gains do not fix the global dependency. The unchanged
pose correctly performs zero rebuilding. This avenue is retained as an unsuitable
locality strategy, not quietly substituted into a benchmark with a smaller body.
The initial log timed full geometry without its rational-moment integration;
that unmatched timing is preserved but excluded. Final logs measure both workloads.

2. LOCALIZED PHYSICAL SUBTRACTION
The alternative clips only the remaining interior of each background seed
against the six box planes, accumulating its outside pieces. An EXACT empty
intersection returns the original uncut seed. Far fluid is no longer partitioned
by infinitely extended solid faces. No tolerance, occupancy voxel approximation,
feature-size pruning, tiny-cell merging or geometric coordinate weld is used.

A deterministic exact-coordinate pulling order provides reproducible local
triangulations. Bounds derive from rational intersections of the finite six
planes. An update stages only seeds overlapping the OLD or NEW solid bounds,
then atomically publishes immutable shared slice ownership. Unaffected slice
objects remain identical. Invalid/degenerate input is rejected without replacing
old owners; an unchanged pose performs no work. Bounds are conservative, so
unnecessary candidates are allowed, but no physically affected endpoint seed
may be skipped. Continuous swept-path discovery is a SEPARATE future requirement.

3. NONMATCHING INTERFACES — VERIFIED, NOT IGNORED
MortarGeometry constructs actual open traces on shared background triangles.
It subtracts the exact solid footprint even when a solid merely touches a face;
a zero-volume seed/body intersection must not accidentally open a blocked passage.
Each pair of nonmatching face subdivisions receives an exact rational2D common
refinement. On BOTH sides, summed overlap moments equal the owner moments exactly
through degree2: integral(1,u,v,u*u,u*v,v*v). These moments are the geometric basis
for conservative affine flux/pressure interface integration. Physical projected-
area/vector-normal conversion remains an assembly responsibility.

MortarTests:10 assertions across four poses/configurations,270 interfaces each:
- Axis-aligned and ordinary oblique body; truly nonmatching subdivisions.
- Solid cap exactly at a cell face: its quarter-area footprint is blocked and
  the remaining aperture is exactly3/4. No zero-volume-touch leakage.
- A tiny solid wholly inside a seed still creates nonmatching exterior traces;
  the test explicitly requires such a case, not merely equal tessellations.
-8–16 nonmatching interfaces and538–719 positive overlap patches per fixture.
- Dropping one actual trace triangle is rejected in every fixture.
This validates geometric interface coverage/moments, NOT pressure/mass-flow
publication or flow across interfaces. No unconnected triangle is treated as a
wall and no water is teleported to bridge unmatched geometry.

4. DISPLACEMENT / WORK / MOMENTUM REFERENCE
ExchangeTests:49 assertions. A separate12-plane Boolean common refinement of the
OLD and NEW body partitions fluid into common capacity, lost capacity (newly
occupied by solid), gained capacity (newly vacated by solid), and common solid.
For translation, cell crossing, rotation and return, exact rational sums recover
both endpoint volumes AND first moments, with nonnegative lost/gained capacity.
Closed-form parallelepiped moments derived independently from the six planes
verify physical capacity. All finite plane coefficients are taken exactly; tiny
rounding-induced differences from an ideal orthonormal box are not repaired.

For uniform gravity(1.25,-9.81,.375), the linear hydrostatic-potential work identity
 rho*g dot delta(M_fluid) = -rho*g dot delta(M_solid)
holds exactly. Prescribed affine-carrier momentum integrated over the gained and
lost capacity likewise equals its endpoint moment difference exactly. This is a
geometric work/carrier-momentum reference under prescribed motion, NOT a solved
body trajectory, dynamic energy test or conservative momentum-advection scheme.
M54 quantity ownership is not modified by newly available CAPACITY.

Two important negative controls:
- Copying only the common overlap loses .0117–.0375m^3 in these moves, even though
  a correct transport must retain owned water and find physical outflow/inflow.
- Out-and-back motion has ZERO net endpoint difference but nonzero leg exchanges
  (sum of lost capacities .079288m^3). Endpoints cannot replace wall-path/swept
  information. The reference intentionally exposes this rather than claiming GCL.
