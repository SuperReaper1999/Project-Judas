DECISION
Do not replace the qualified Stage14 implementation with this experiment.
The reformulation is numerically correct in the tested cases, but DOES NOT make
moving fluids practical. Its complete measured moving update remains1.06s median,

ACTUAL IMPLEMENTATION
Stage14 first eliminates the independent local continuity equations by a pivot
coordinate parameterization, then QR-factorizes the reduced kinetic matrix J.
That parameterization introduces dense coupling into otherwise sparse columns.
The experiment instead factors the ORIGINAL sparse internal kinetic matrix Ai
and keeps the few independent continuity constraints separate and orthogonal.
All coefficients remain long double; only exact zeros are omitted.

For Ai P=Q R, define W=D_i P R^-1 and factor W^T=U T with orthonormal U.
For raw source residual r and required internal divergence g, the minimum kinetic
residual is
 ||Q_tail^T r||^2 + ||U^T Q_head^T r - T^-T g||^2.
This supplies the condensed boundary/body/source operators without eliminating
continuity through kinetic coordinates. Full internal motion is reconstructed
by projecting Q_head^T r onto W y=g and triangular-solving R.

The same two accurate-gradient refinement steps remain. Their preconditioner
uses R^-1 (I-U U^T) R^-T with the correct permutation, then extracts the original
free coordinates. Dependent coordinates still reconstruct from actual continuity
equations. No damping, energy clamp, added diagonal, dropped cell/mode, shortened
physics cadence or acceptance-budget increase was introduced.

The implementation is archived under fluid-orthogonal-constraints-stage15-20261029.
It reuses preceding geometry, field assembly, independent oracles and native probe
infrastructure. It has NOT been wired into Judas or substituted for Stage14.

BOUNDED NEXT ENGINEERING DIRECTION
Stop treating another QR micro-optimization as the route to readiness. Preserve
this variational/high-precision implementation as a correctness reference.
Investigate a bounded runtime cut-cell representation whose unknown count and
operator construction do not scale with every transient triangulation fragment.
Evaluate it against independent hydrostatic/buoyancy, work, momentum and exact
conservation contracts, and matched moving workloads BEFORE production adoption.
Explicitly distinguish discretization error from algebraic solver error; do not
silently weaken existing fixtures or pretend a new approximation is bit-equivalent.
The previous aggregate/low-precision failures must inform that design.

In parallel the actual time-dependent contract remains unsolved: swept wall/GCL,
connected displaced-water transport, conservative momentum, evolving wet/dry and
