IMPLEMENTED NATIVE BOUNDARY
--------------------------
PhysicsConstraintBatch.h exposes bounded copied native bodies, actual contact
Jacobians/normal-speed targets, and identified bilateral joint rows. A native
participant submits material wrenches and matching native reactions. The engine
computes physical velocities, validates finite values, complementarity, joint
constraints, identities and lifetimes, then acknowledges staged material state.
No internal native dispatch or foreign authoritative pose assignment is needed.
See docs/PHYSICAL_CONSTRAINT_BATCH.md for exact restrictions and failure semantics.

WorldConstraintBatch.cpp owns snapshot/validation/publication. MK2 remains owner
of contact history, native integration, CCD and ordering. JointSolver can record
already-applied reactions without applying warm impulses twice. The optional
interface has no JS binding and is absent from ordinary project configuration.
The default no-participant route retains eight byte-identical native trajectory
workloads (960 outer steps) against the committed Stage5 reference.
