CONTINUOUS SHADOW FILTERING — EXPERIMENTAL, 2 NOVEMBER 2026

Baseline: f60c5a4cb5b37c43423269818de993bf87c893ad.
This is a rendering correction, not a release, fluid unlock or desktop-crash fix.
Main and published packages are unchanged. Human acceptance is pending.

CONFIRMED MECHANISM
The existing receiver-plane correction is present and passes its sloped-plane
controls. However, its nine equal-weight nearest depth comparisons select their
whole footprint with floor(projectedUV * mapSize). Crossing a texel boundary
replaces an entire column/row at once. A one-dimensional edge therefore jumps
by 1/3 in visibility. This appears as blocky, snapping shadow edges on the pool,
ordinary boxes and steps; it is not specific to water.

The desktop directional map spans 50 m at 1024 squared (about 4.88 cm/texel).
That remains a finite-resolution shadow representation, especially visible
close to a receiver. This patch does not claim unlimited shadow detail.

CORRECTION
Interpolate the comparison results of four adjacent 3x3 kernel placements.
Their shared samples form a 4x4 footprint, with per-axis weights
  [1 - phase, 1, 1, phase], phase = fract(projectedUV * mapSize - 0.5).
The two weight sums are each 3, so total normalization remains 9.
Every tap still uses the actual receiver-plane depth at that texel center.
Raw depth values are not interpolated, which would fabricate occluder depths.
Existing bias, map size/count, directional coverage, lighting and geometry are
unchanged. Sampling work is 16 depth fetches per evaluated light versus 9;
shadow-map memory/resolution are unchanged. No throughput speedup is claimed.

REJECTED AUTHORING EXPERIMENT
The original stairs do have 13 overlapping box pairs. Partitioning the identical
solid into adjoining slabs removed internal overlap while preserving the exact
outer union, rise, footprint and all 1440 water positions. But the two captured
cameras changed only 0 and 6 shadowed pixels respectively out of 760000; the
unshadowed captures changed 0 and 3 pixels. It did not explain the reported
pattern. That experiment is preserved and not integrated. The project content,
fluid parameters and collision geometry remain byte-identical to the baseline.
The stair shape genuinely casts diagonal wedges; smoothing those shadows must
not be confused with removing legitimate occlusion.
