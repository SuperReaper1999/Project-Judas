IMPLEMENTED
Stage11 established an independent oracle for the worst local thin-cut problem.
Stage12 applies that correction to EVERY local patch and reconstructs dependent
as well as free-face flows. Geometry, mass, constraints, source definitions,
physical timestep and acceptance tolerances are unchanged. No cell is removed,
no fluid is clamped, no damping is added and no native physics budget changes.

The private RefinedRuntime owns original fixed-geometry response operators,
value-owned compensated sparse gradient coefficients, local triangular factors
and physical flux scales. It evaluates g=J^T(r-Jx) with paired-binary64 arithmetic
and applies two corrections through the existing QR factors. Dependent flows
are then rebuilt from the actual continuity equations, not repaired afterward.
Unsupported arithmetic and nonconverged final correction are explicit failures.
The existing final continuity(1e-10), reaction(1e-8), kinetic-work(1e-9) checks
remain. The last correction must be finite and below1e-12 in physical free flux.
This correction-size check is a runtime refusal criterion, not a universal
forward-error certificate; the independent accuracy tests remain essential.

No tracked production implementation/API, Basic Water, M54/M55 or experimental
policy changes. Alpha remains compiled-locked. Independent fluid research is
untouched. These sources are reproducible archived prototypes, not a new runtime
backend hidden behind an enabled project flag.

WHAT STILL BLOCKS AN ALPHA UNLOCK
- Moving-solid geometry epochs, incremental invalidation and geometric conservation
  with real displaced-volume transport, without per-step cold reconstruction.
- Conservative momentum advection and genuinely evolving free surfaces/wet-dry.
- General native contacts/friction/impacts and constraints beyond bounded proofs.
- Full pressure-model/trace approximation accuracy and broader conditioning/rank
  coverage. Two refinement steps are not a proof for arbitrary future geometry.
- Practical total runtime/memory, native precision portability and M54 ownership.
- Whole-application persistence, streaming/export and the original Terrain planet
  failure, including repeated physical contact without launches.
This checkpoint closes the all-local reconstruction question, not those systems.
