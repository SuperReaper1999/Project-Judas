CONSERVATION AND KINETIC EQUATIONS
-----------------------------------
Let q denote relative face flux coordinates, V the physical rigid velocity,
Aq + BV the wet-fluid mass-root velocity, and b its mass-root predictor.
The reference minimizes
  1/2 ||Aq + BV - b||^2
subject to
  Dq + Eb V = 0.
D integrates divergence against (1,x,y,z) over each exactly connected wet piece
of an original background tetrahedron. The constant moment is the actual material
volume balance; the other moments permit the hydrostatic potential and preserve
compatibility with the reconstructed field.

The coupled native variant additionally includes the actual native rigid mass
and inertia in the kinetic functional and the actual prepared bilateral joint
rows as constraints. Rigid mass is included ONCE. The fluid reaction is the
variation of the same fluid kinetic/constraint functional with respect to V:
  I_fluid = B^T (b - Aq - BV) - Eb^T lambda.
The native solver receives this material impulse plus the recovered native joint
impulses. It retains integration, CCD, contact history and physical ownership.
No private native dispatch, direct body-velocity publication, fake boundary
