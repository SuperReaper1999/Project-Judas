WHAT NOW WORKS
The localized geometry is connected to an actual conservative variational
pressure solve, not merely a geometric overlap report. Shared affine normal
traces join nonmatching background faces; local kinetic coordinates are condensed
and reconstructed, and physical continuity, work, force and energy are checked.
Unchanged local geometry/factors are reused after prescribed pose changes, with
bit-identical cached-versus-fresh matrices and physical results in the measured
fixtures. Correctness is independently checked rather than inferred from reuse.

The qualified private entry is QualifiedPressure.h / QualifiedEpoch. It uses
SparsePressureEpoch.h and the SAME long-double coefficients and numerical bounds
as the dense reference. The dense path remains preserved as an independent
implementation/performance reference. The faster double-QR experiment is rejected.
No tracked engine/API/build wiring, project assets, Basic Water, M54/M55, FLU-001
or release policy changed. Alpha's compiled fluid lock remains intact. Independent
research and all previous frozen evidence are untouched.

DISCRETIZATION / INTERFACES
Mass/kinetic integration and four macro continuity moments(1,x,y,z) retain the
Stage9–12 affine H(div)/normal-flux formulation. Fine tetrahedra supply velocity
reconstruction and integration; this is NOT a new claim of pointwise zero
divergence in every fine tetrahedron or proven continuum convergence.

Each open background triangle has one shared affine RELATIVE normal trace,
used on both sides despite different micro-triangulations. This is the same
coarse trace-space restriction as the earlier macro approach; it does not promise
arbitrary independent piecewise traces. Prior exact common-refinement moments
validate geometry/trace coverage. The new disconnected-interface control evaluates
actual squared flux jumps using those degree2 aperture moments.

For a pressure-bearing seed, local plane arrangement is made conforming INSIDE
that seed. This is important: Stage13's minimal outside-piece subtraction can
also have nonmatching internal faces and cannot simply be passed into the old
matching-face finite-element table. Only seeds overlapping the exact conservative
solid bounds receive that local arrangement; distant seeds stay uncut. Touching
seeds are included so blocked boundary footprints have proper local ownership.
There is no geometric epsilon, tiny-cell pruning, coordinate welding or dropped
kinetic mode. The new interior discretization is deliberately declared.

OWNERSHIP / FRAME / PUBLICATION
Immutable local factors hold geometry-dependent kinetic, constraint and trace
operators and prepared source/trace/body responses. Epoch updates stage affected
geometry, factors, interface numbering and global preparation before replacing
old data. Source evaluation is separate and read-only. Invalid geometry, NaN,
finite kinetic overflow, unsupported rounding and unsupported domains reject
without mutating the prior epoch. Empty fluid seeds are explicitly handled;
fully displaced domains currently reject with a useful diagnostic.

The rigid-velocity lifting uses a FIXED reference at local coordinate0, rather
than the moving COM. Otherwise a COM change would spuriously invalidate distant
body-lift blocks. Helpers explicitly map v_origin=v_COM-omega cross COM and
 torque_COM=torque_origin-COM cross force.
A non-axis power identity is tested. Native callers must perform those transforms;
this is not a new JudasJS/native public binding or claimed native integration.
