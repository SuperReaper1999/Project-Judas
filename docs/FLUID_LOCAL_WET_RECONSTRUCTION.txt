Why the previous approaches failed
---------------------------------
A global halo ban fixed the dry cavities but broke four existing player checks.
Strict actual-footprint-only admission preserved dry space but left holes in the
disordered PBF samples and failed neutral drift. Bounding the old halo by another
vertical threshold kept nominal-bin false positives and missing-bin negatives.
Those failures remain in the preceding checkpoint's evidence, unchanged.

The replacement reconstructs small gaps directly between actual local samples.
It does not fill a connected patch's whole convex hull: a ring of water may
surround a genuine dry cavity. Unrelated coarse particles cannot enlarge the
support assigned to an existing local pair.

Mathematical model and numeric representation
--------------------------------------------
Particle spacing si=cbrt(mi/rho), halfwidth hi=si/2, as in the existing sampler.
Positions, masses and input settings remain binary32. Query-frame subtraction,
normalization, affine clipping, square roots and interval integration use
ordinary binary64. No extended/113-bit/rational arithmetic or research solver.

In the query's tangent/up/bitangent frame, raw particle cell:
  Ci = ci + hi[-1,1]^3.
For two nearby samples, f=min(si,sj), Ri=hi+f/2, Rj=hj+f/2. Interpolated support:
  conv(Ci,Cj) intersect Ball(ci,Ri-Ei) intersect Ball(cj,Rj-Ej),
where effective radii are nonnegative and Ei is the existing form of local
binary32 input-position uncertainty. Ri+Rj=hi+hj+f; equal-size particles retain
the one-spacing unresolved gap scale, without a new authoring knob.

For c(t)=ci+t(cj-ci), h(t)=hi+t(hj-hi), 0<=t<=1, four affine inequalities
  -h(t)<=cx(t)<=h(t), -h(t)<=cz(t)<=h(t)
give the part of the pair hull intersecting the query ray. Its minimum lower-Y
and maximum upper-Y values are taken at the feasible interval endpoints. Each
sphere independently clips that ray by cy +/- sqrt(R_effective^2-cx^2-cz^2).
Raw and pair intervals are clipped to the requested height and unioned by exact
overlap/touch only. There is no additional gap closure or double counting.

Raw cubes remain authoritative. Only interpolation is uncertainty-eroded. The
same effective radius controls both the tangency predicate AND the square root;
otherwise an O(epsilon) coordinate error becomes O(sqrt(epsilon)) false water.
A pair must have positive ray length before clipping, preserving valid H=0
queries without inventing a wet point from a zero-volume touching lens.

Candidate collection separately tests the actual/uncertain raw footprint and
the particle's own compact support ball. A body-sized donor radius, or another
particle's rounding allowance, cannot enlarge an existing local connection.
The public interface, project settings, save format and solver budgets are unchanged.

Rejected new experiments are preserved
-------------------------------------
- Hard pair-hull activation removes a finite water prism at2si separation. A
  known-answer sweep gives1->1->0, despite an infinitesimal separation change.
- Intersecting the pair hull with its two support balls instead shrinks that
  contribution to zero. At the same sweep it gives .00199999975->0->0.
- Native-v1 passed the34 root checks and neutral/swim controls but failed four
  rotated vertical-support assertions. A nominally tangent neighboring support
  sphere gained a tiny positive radicand after float rotation.
- Moving only the tangency guard inward still leaves a finite jump at that new
  guard. Eroding the sphere used by BOTH predicate and sqrt fixes this.
- Merging intervals with the largest input error from unrelated off-ray cells
  could close another layer's air gap. The final union uses exact touching only.
No artificial damping, force/energy clamps, settling delays or test retuning.

Limits and remaining work
-------------------------
This is bounded interpolation for games, not literal connected water geometry,
a conserved-volume pressure solve or an independently reconstructed continuum.
Particle mass is unchanged; filling sampling gaps does not create new particles.
Local lenses can be disconnected from raw cubes at large unresolved separations.
Sub-resolution pockets and exact multiphase topology remain outside this model.

Pair-support birth/death is continuous in the tested finite-height controls.
That is not a promise of universal force continuity at every raw-cube boundary,
quadrature boundary or curved-cap/ownership transition. Binary32 world-coordinate
precision still limits fine details; the arithmetic upgrade does not create
planet-scale precision or support active-fluid live origin shifts.

The public force controls independently check analytic ray sections and wiring
through the existing one-sample quadrature. They do not prove exact continuum
integration of an entire body, pressure accuracy, global volume or energy
conservation. The48 engine/80 observer known-geometry failures remain distinct
from the now-passing root-admission contract. Historical settling speed failures
and warm-up diagnostics are preserved. Alpha remains locked pending broader work.
Active PBF cold saves, regional suspension and live origin shifts remain unsupported.
No Windows, Android, browser or new human qualification is claimed.
