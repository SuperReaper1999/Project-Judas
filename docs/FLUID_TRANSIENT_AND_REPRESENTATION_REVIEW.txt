Established production mechanisms:
- FluidWorld.h defines particleRadius as preferred SOFT clearance; physical
  exclusion applies to the actual particle centre. Unavailable skins can yield.
- FluidWorld.cpp retains velocity normals even after a skin projection is
  clipped, and accumulates contact normals across the substep's projections.
- PhysicsWorld::SolveParticleContacts, src/physics/WorldParticles.cpp, converts
  every row to signedSeparation0, losing actual positive gap and skin status.
- Ordinary exterior solids are prescribed/infinite-mass boundaries to water;
  reciprocal finite-mass response is reserved for explicitly owned cavities.
  Their velocity cannot be reduced by these exterior particle impulses.

2. CAUSAL CONTROLS, NOT JUST A CORRELATION
A zero-PBF/gravity one-particle wedge reproduces the high response through actual
SolveParticleContacts. Floor constraint vy>=0 plus a descending inclined wall
normal(0,-cos(theta),sin(theta)), speedU, demands
  -cos(theta)*vy + sin(theta)*vz >= U*cos(theta).
Minimum-energy prescribed-wall solution: vy=0, vz=U*cot(theta), unbounded as
theta approaches0. More iterations can approach a HIGHER speed, not repair it.
For U8m/s and theta11degrees, current64PGS gives37.3303m/s; exact limit41.1564.
Removing the floor gives7.853m/s; stationary wall gives0. A finite2000kg wall
gives15.150m/s, converges and reduces combined kinetic energy from64000J to
25030.8J. These are controlled row fixtures, not a full geometrically authored
pool or proof that all physical squeeze jets should have small velocity.

The ACTUAL captured pair of rows exactly reproduces substep0's recorded result:
  before(-.263729572,-.226283818,-.252897322)
  zero-gap after(1.28645456,-4.29604864,21.162796).
Using the actual positive gaps in the existing ContactSolver instead produces
zero impulse and leaves the original velocity bit-identical. Frozen-normal,
constant-wall-velocity prediction over h=(1/30)/3 seconds remains separated:
  floor gap.125327 -> .122813m; block gap.180000 -> .118827m.
All six captured particle217/242 substep states admit zero new impulse with real
gaps. The old zero-gap rows add large impulses at all six states. Independent
crossing/touch/moving-wall-crossing controls still receive stopping impulses.
The ordinary plane inequality is g+h*n dot(v-wall)>=0, hence target>=-g/h.
The existing solver may deliberately select an instantaneous restitution target
when a real crossing is predicted. No crossing is predicted in these six tests.

This establishes a premature constraint kick under the physical-point model.
It does NOT establish full rotating-box/finite-feature CCD safety or an improved
whole-pool trajectory with a new policy. Later captured states already contain
energy injected earlier; supplying gaps does not erase that energy.

3. CORRECT REPAIR BOUNDARY
Retain genuine hard swept encounters; revalidate finite features and their real
gap at the final position. Distinguish hard impact from feasible preferred skin
and clipped/unavailable preferred clearance. Supply an honest prediction horizon:
fluid substep duration differs from the rigid-support interval in the same batch.
Where preferred skins cannot coexist, their velocity constraints must yield as
their position constraints already do. Handle the incompatible group coherently.
Preserve feasible stand-off behavior: existing SmoothingWall controls require
zero inward velocity at feasible preferred clearance. Globally treating ALL
skin rows as loose point gaps can leave velocity falling while position is held
at the skin. Endpoint gaps alone also cannot discard a genuine earlier swept hit.
No velocity cap, extra damping, larger iteration budget or fake wall velocity.

One-line twoWay=true is not a qualified repair. Exterior hydrostatic buoyancy
and drag are applied separately; applying all contact reactions may count some
pressure/weight twice. The finite-mass control proves why prescribed work matters,
not that blanket reciprocal response is compatible with the current force split.

4. WHAT JUDAS ALREADY HAS WITHOUT PARTICLES
BasicWater.h explicitly supplies prescribed visuals/swimming with no mass ledger,
pressure or rigid coupling. It cannot replace advanced conserved water.
LiquidSystem.h/.cpp (M54) owns double-precision volume transactions, basins,
containers and detached parcels. LiquidPersistence.cpp saves that actual state.
LiquidSurface.h/.cpp (M55) stores per-cell volume and face discharge; its
hydrostatic/local-inertia surface solve pairs debit/credit transfers. It is a
real conserved bulk-water model, not a general3D pressure/advection solve.
docs/LIQUID_SURFACES.md explicitly limits it to long-wave/hydrostatic behavior:
no nonlinear momentum advection or overturning sheets; cup response is
quasi-static/approximate. It is reusable for calmer bulk-water gameplay, but
cannot silently replace unrestricted3D pouring/slosh/jet behavior.

5. REPLACEMENT OPTIONS AND ENGINEERING RECOMMENDATION
Conserved surface water:
  Highest immediate reuse through M54/M55. Suitable for filling/draining,
  water levels, moderate waves and authored volume-transfer/container workflows.
  It restricts3D motion. Its moving-solid clipping/submersion cost still needs
  measurement; existing implementation does not prove it faster in this pool.
Bounded staggered3D grid with conservative volume fractions:
  Strongest non-particle candidate for retaining freely moving3D water. Store
  occupied quantity and pressure in cells and velocities/fluxes on faces. Make
  actual solid cuts/face apertures and pressure/body reaction part of one coherent
  update. Sample occupancy and build the surface from this same owned state.
  Replaces PBF density/contact reconstruction instead of retaining it underneath.
  Coarse-grid cut geometry, pressure coupling, transport and thin/disconnected
  subcell features remain real engineering work. Performance is NOT established.
PIC/FLIP/APIC:
  Hybrid particles plus grid; may improve pressure organization but still has
  particles/transfers/surface reconstruction. Not a purely non-particle answer.
Unstructured tetrahedral/FEM fluid:
  Can fit boundaries but introduces remeshing/operator/solver costs. Existing
  M54 tetrahedra are storage geometry, not a ready3D dynamic fluid solver.

For the existing free3D gameplay goals, first correct the demonstrated contact
contract, then qualify a SMALL bounded volume-grid prototype before any backend
replacement. Keep the accepted M54/M55 and Basic Water implementations intact.
M54's ownership/persistence concepts and existing gravity/geometry/rigid/render
APIs are reuse candidates, not a second competing water quantity owner.
Use surface-water where its explicit scope fits, not as an undisclosed downgrade.

Why a grid is promising here: current cost includes repeated neighborhood/density
passes in FluidWorld.cpp, separate hydrostatic reconstruction/integration in
ProductionFluidCoupling.cpp, and sparse particle scatter/surface extraction in
FluidSurface.cpp. Removing particle storage alone would retain much of the cost
if those reconstructions were left in place. A new grid must expose its owned
geometry/quantity directly. No matched replacement benchmark exists yet.

Initial prototype comparison boundary:
- Same12x8m basin, physical volume180m3/mass180000kg, solids, gravity, throws,
  physics cadence and quality. Do not fit a new backend to PBF's settled height.
- A12x8x3m core at.5m spacing has2304cells before halos/boundary subdivision;
  at.25m it has18432. Counts alone do not establish cost or equivalent accuracy.
- Analytic hydrostatic force/torque, neutral/light/heavy bodies, dry gaps,
  oblique walls, current squeeze episode, moving/rotating cups and pour/receive
  quantity accounting. Thin walls and disconnected water inside one coarse cell
  need real ownership/topology, refinement or honest unsupported admission.
- Measure fluid solve, solid coupling, queries, meshing and memory separately,
  using identical physical acceptance gates and no cheaper hidden collider set.
- Preserve angular/linear momentum and work accounting for the SUPPORTED model;
  prove failed updates cannot publish partial quantity. Persistence/origin/
  streaming support requires explicit state/admission contracts, not inheritance
  by assertion from M54. Retain original failures and negative controls.
