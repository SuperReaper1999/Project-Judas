# Lunar Island Stage 5 — human review

Stage 5 is pending operator review. Start a new Stage 5 expedition; earlier-stage
slots remain preserved under different save identities. Stage 6 is not authorized.

1. Open M. Choose Whisperwood and Track location. Toggle Reference map and back;
   confirm the guide is clear and selecting a destination never teleports you.
2. Take fuel/repair supplies from the homestead locker, load a vehicle and follow
   the forest road west from south of the airstrip. Drive the branches to town,
   works, city and harbour. Judge hill handling and route readability yourself.
3. Walk off the road into a forest patch. Fell/process timber and mine stone/ore.
   Leave far enough for unloading and return; depleted resources must stay depleted.
4. Visit Moonbridge. Loot a chest, open a real hinged door, barricade a window and
   move furniture. Store supplies, leave the district, return and check the changes.
5. Rest in a bed to claim a home. Leave and recover there when needed. Claiming a
   different bed or dismantling it should release the original home's residency pin.
6. Talk/trade with Rowan, Asha and Ellis. Deliver real requested resources. Try a
   failed trade/reward with a full pack: inventory and money must not disappear.
7. Approach Blackwater Works and the civic quarter. Hostiles should detect, pursue
   and fight on the authored streets. Defeat one, loot its physical remains, leave
   and return. No resurrected loot/enemy or duplicated rewards should appear.
8. Recover radio components from the city and deliver them to Nia. Repeat the
   completed dialogue: the reward must not repeat. Regional kills must not satisfy
   Mara's specific homestead camp-clearing objective.
9. Go down Tidemark's slipway. Board the skiff from the pier. Confirm ocean-level
   flotation, cargo and controls. Park beside the dock and E: prefer a dry exit.
10. Reach Lantern Islet by boat or helicopter. Inspect the separate terrain, mineral
    deposits, refuge, supplies and lighthouse. The tower is a sealed landmark; the
    furnished refuge is enterable. Check the islet ground does not flicker.
11. F6 with modified districts behind you. Quit completely, relaunch and F9. Verify
    inventory, location guide, depleted resources, storage, door/barricade physics,
    NPC death/loot and vehicle cargo/fuel. Continue playing afterward.
12. Open the editable project and Play/Stop; authored content should be restored.
    Try the relocated standalone and listen to audio yourself.

resident coarse terrain shell; bounded district navigation; sealed landmark façades;
