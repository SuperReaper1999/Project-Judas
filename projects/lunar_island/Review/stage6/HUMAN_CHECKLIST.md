# Lunar Island Stage 6 — final operator review

Stage 5 was reviewed with the helicopter complaint carried into this candidate.
Stage 6 gameplay/visual acceptance is pending. Automation is not operator acceptance.
Use a fresh Stage 6 expedition; earlier-stage save slots are preserved separately.

1. Start, read the shorter welcome and open Controls. Switch to Vehicles & travel
   and back. Change sensitivity; pause/resume and confirm capture and look behave.
2. Gather → craft axe/pick → fell/process a tree → mine → cook → refill/drink.
   Check clear feedback for missing materials, wrong station and a full pack.
3. Loot/transfer supplies, build a physical piece, secure an opening, move furniture
   and rest at a claimed bed. Follow a survivor quest through an actual reward.
4. Drive a pickup with cargo along a regional road. Enter a furnished town house,
   change it, depart and return. Check the original changes and regional remains.
5. Pilot the helicopter: allow spin-up, Space climb, W forward, A/D yaw. Confirm
   the faster cruise feels useful, and watch the fuselage relative to the camera
   at your normal frame rate. Release W and judge the return to hover.
6. Look around and toggle V during flight. Land with Ctrl, release near ground,
   then E exit. The player should be visible in third person and collision intact.
7. Try the plane, spring/ocean skiff and a road vehicle; inspect cargo/refuel/repair.
8. M → Track location, switch reference image/list and Stop tracking. The journal
   should return without teleporting or forgetting exploration discoveries.
9. Save in flight or with modified regions behind you. Wait for saved confirmation,
   quit entirely and reload. Inspect real aircraft/body/door state, cargo, fuel,
   depleted resources, NPC death, storage and inventory; then continue playing.
10. Move the whole standalone folder and use `Run Lunar Island.sh` from elsewhere.
    Check rendering, mouse/keyboard and listen to sound yourself.
11. Open the project in the editor, inspect a region and Play/Stop. Follow HANDOVER.md
    to change a disposable item/flight value, export, and test with a disposable save.
12. Review the actual remaining-content and animation boundaries in HANDOVER.md.
    This is a playable foundation, not a completed commercial-scale survival RPG.

Do not infer Windows/Android, physical-controller, clean-machine or audio-listening
acceptance from the Linux automated checks. Main promotion requires explicit approval.
