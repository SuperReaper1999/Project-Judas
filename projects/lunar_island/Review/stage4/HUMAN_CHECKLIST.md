# Lunar Island Stage 4 — human review

Human acceptance is pending. Earlier stages were reviewed separately. Start a new
Stage 4 expedition; old-stage saves are deliberately kept under different identities.

1. From camp walk south: E on the blue car → Drive. WASD, Space brake; mouse orbit,
   V chase/bonnet. Stop and E to get out. Confirm no camera is inside the avatar.
2. E on the supply locker beside the vehicles → Cargo. Take a fuel can and repair
   kit. Refuel a vehicle with 5 L of space; check that a full tank refuses without
   consuming the can. Try repairing an undamaged vehicle: no kit should be consumed.
3. Gather timber, open the green pickup's cargo, load it, drive somewhere clear,
   stop, unload and build with it. Check personal/cargo weights and no duplication.
4. Try an overweight transfer. It should explain the failure and leave both bags
   intact. Try the motorcycle's smaller saddlebags and the other cargo capacities.
5. Drive the motorcycle over uneven ground. It has balance assistance, not a
   simulated human rider. All road vehicles have real native wheels/suspension.
6. Find the spring dock north-east. E on the skiff → Pilot. W/S thrust, A/D rudder,
   Space water brake. Stop beside the dock to exit; swimming still works if wet.
7. Fly the yellow helicopter west of the apron. Space climbs, Ctrl descends,
   W/S tilt and A/D yaw. Land gently, let it settle, then E to exit.
8. Walk south along the short runway to the plane. Hold W to raise throttle; gain
   speed then Space to lift off. Ctrl lowers pitch, A/D turns; S reduces throttle.
   Land on clear ground, Shift wheel-brake, stop and E. Assisted flight is deliberate.
9. Damage a vehicle by a low-speed impact or a firearm. Confirm condition/power
   changes, then stop beside it and use Repair. Cargo must survive damage/repair.
10. Use Mara's Trade menu for additional fuel/kits; make a repair kit at a workbench
    from three scrap and one plank. Stock is finite; no fuel production is claimed.
11. F6 while parked/piloting with cargo. Quit completely, relaunch and F9. Confirm
    actual vehicle location, fuel, condition, cargo and pilot control; drive again.
12. The extra pickup east of the apron belongs to a small garage region. Change
    its cargo without driving, leave about 200 m and return. Cargo should persist.
    Driving adopts the whole assembly into persistent root ownership; it no longer
    unloads at the original garage boundary.
13. Pause while driving: look/throttle/fire must not leak through the menu. Stop
    editor Play: authored vehicles should return to their original scene state.
14. Test the relocated standalone, engine/rotor sounds, menu readability and controls.

Known boundaries: cargo weight limits do not change chassis mass; no passengers,
walkable vehicle interiors, seated avatar animation, deforming car panels, full
flight simulator, populated towns or automatic re-suspension of driven vehicles.
Rotor blades are visual; primary body collision is a coarse physical proxy.
