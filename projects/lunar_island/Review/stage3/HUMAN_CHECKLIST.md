# Stage 3 human playthrough

Automated Linux checks are separate from this pending operator review.

1. Launch the project or standalone. Wait for the visible preparation screen, then
   Begin. Move/look, jump, V first/third person; menus should release the pointer
   and block movement/fire. Check sound volume and feedback with real audio.
2. E near Mara at camp → Ask about work. Receive one pistol/24 rounds. Repeat the
   dialogue: no duplicate gift. Tab → Backpack → select pistol → Use / equip.
3. R reload; right mouse aim; left click fire. Check recoil, reticle alignment,
   physical hit feedback and finite magazine/reserve. A shot at Mara must not hurt her.
4. Gather six timber and speak to Ivo → Ask about work. Receive 15 tokens/two rations
   once. Open either survivor's trade page. Buy/sell one item; check finite stock
   and money. Select pistol ammunition and try Buy ten rounds. Insufficient money
   or pack space must spend nothing.
5. Approach the eastern camp (about 75,66). Check lookout shooting, raider pursuit,
   occlusion by terrain/cover, close attacks and low-health retreat. Clear both,
   E on a corpse to loot. Return to Mara for the one-time 35-token/two-dressing reward.
6. Buy knife, shotgun/shells and rifle/rounds as funds allow. Test distinct melee,
   held rifle fire, timed reload, empty magazines and shotgun pellets. Switch V
   without losing health, weapon, ammo or progress; third-person aiming should align.
7. Hunt the two western wolves. E their bodies for finite meat/hide; cook meat at
   the fire. Judge simple movement/attack presentation: wolf gait animation and
   weapon-specific human animations remain explicit art gaps.
8. Build a wood barricade across a cottage window using two planks/one nail.
   Alert a nearby enemy with a shot and shelter behind it. Actual near attacks can
   break the board; distant/unalerted enemies must not damage it. This is not an
   intelligent squad siege or dynamic navmesh-rebake system.
9. F6 save, quit completely and relaunch/F9. Check money, inventory, magazines,
   quests, NPC stock/deaths/loot and corpse positions. Repeat a completed quest:
   it must remain completed. Check the same save with the relocated standalone.
10. Existing harvesting, building, storage, home/bed, doors and basic swimming
    should still work. Editor Stop restores authored state; game saves are separate.

Known presentation limits: rigid proxy corpses, static wolf legs, simple human
attack clip, no voice dialogue or third-person weapon-specific held/aim poses.
Vehicles and wider settlement population belong to later, unauthorized stages.
