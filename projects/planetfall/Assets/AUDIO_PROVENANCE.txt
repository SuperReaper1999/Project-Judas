Lastlight reuses the original Project Judas M52 shot/impact PCM effects. New rifle_fire, reload_click, melee_thud, empty_click and explosion effects are original deterministic synthesis, recorded in Tools/combat-audio.json; no external recordings or samples are used. These effects are distributed under the repository license. DejaVu font licensing is preserved in fonts/LICENSE.txt. No external music or sound library is used.
