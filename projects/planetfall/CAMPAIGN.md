## Side contracts

Cycle with P. Scarred Bearings requires native permanent deformation or physical interface failure, followed by committed dismantling of the damaged section. The Long Way Home uses the physical rover/bridge. A Better View requires reaching the research roof. Salvage Rights and A Quiet Landing spawn independent physical combat encounters.

## Final choice

At the final council, the Journal’s “Contain the crystal” button contains it and improves healing near Lantern. “Reconnect the network” reconnects it, reducing interplanetary fuel costs. Neither choice moves planets or implements a new gravity solver.
